## Search intent

Primary intent: a Chrome user wants to activate PiP for a video, understand the built-in controls, know when the official Google extension helps, and fix a missing or unstable PiP window.

Secondary intent: a developer or advanced user wants to distinguish normal video PiP from Document Picture-in-Picture. This is explained as a web-platform capability that a participating site must implement, not as a consumer switch that forces arbitrary websites to detach content.

Out of scope: a universal list of streaming services, claims that every website supports PiP, claims about mobile/iOS behavior without a browser-specific source, and instructions that bypass protected playback or a site’s terms.
