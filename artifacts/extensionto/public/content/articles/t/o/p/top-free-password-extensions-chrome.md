# Top Free Password Extensions for Chrome (2026) — Ranked

This is a straightforward ranking of the best completely free password extensions for Chrome — tested for autofill quality, security, and honest free-tier restrictions.

## Ranking Criteria

Each extension was evaluated on:
1. **Security** — Encryption standard, zero-knowledge, independent audit
2. **Free tier completeness** — No crippled autofill or device limits
3. **Chrome extension quality** — Autofill reliability, phishing protection
4. **Privacy** — No data collection, no ads

---

## #1 Bitwarden Chrome Extension

**Free tier:** Unlimited passwords, unlimited devices, full autofill
**Security:** AES-256, Argon2id, zero-knowledge, Cure53 audit
**Chrome extension rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

The clear top pick. Bitwarden's Chrome extension is polished, reliable, and covers all features on the free plan. Domain-matched autofill prevents phishing. Password generator, secure notes, and credit card storage all included free.

## #2 KeePassXC-Browser Extension

**Free tier:** Everything — 100% free, local storage
**Security:** AES-256 / ChaCha20, FOSSA audited
**Chrome extension rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Requires KeePassXC desktop app running alongside Chrome. Autofill works via a secure native messaging channel. Slightly more setup effort but maximum privacy — no data ever leaves your device.

## #3 Proton Pass Chrome Extension

**Free tier:** Unlimited passwords, unlimited devices, email aliases
**Security:** AES-256, end-to-end encrypted, Cure53 audit
**Chrome extension rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Proton Pass launched its stable Chrome extension in 2024. Autofill reliability improved significantly through 2025. Unique free feature: integrated email alias generation.

## #4 NordPass Chrome Extension

**Free tier:** Unlimited passwords, 1 active device at a time
**Security:** XChaCha20, zero-knowledge
**Chrome extension rating:** ⭐⭐⭐

Free tier limitation: only one device can be active (logged in) simultaneously. Switching devices requires logging out and back in. Acceptable for single-device users.

## FAQ

**Q: Which free password extension has the best autofill?**
Bitwarden — its URL matching is the most configurable and reliable of any free option. It supports domain, host, starts-with, exact match, and regex matching modes.

**Q: Is Proton Pass as trustworthy as Bitwarden?**
Both are audited and zero-knowledge. Proton's privacy track record (ProtonMail) is excellent. Bitwarden has more years of audit history. Both are trustworthy choices.
