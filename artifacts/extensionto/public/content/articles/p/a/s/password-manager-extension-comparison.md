## Category Winners

**Best free tier:** Bitwarden (unlimited passwords, unlimited devices, full features)
**Best security architecture:** 1Password (Secret Key) or KeePass (local only)
**Best for developers:** KeePass or Bitwarden (both have CLI, KeePass has SSH agent)
**Best for teams:** 1Password or Bitwarden Organizations
**Best value paid:** Bitwarden ($10/year — unmatched)
**Best for maximum privacy:** KeePass (zero cloud, zero telemetry)
**Best UI/UX:** 1Password or Dashlane
**Best Passkey management:** 1Password or Bitwarden

## Head-to-Head: Bitwarden vs 1Password

The two most recommended options in 2026:

| | Bitwarden | 1Password |
|---|---|---|
| Free tier | ✅ Full unlimited | ❌ Trial only |
| Price | $10/year | $35.88/year |
| Open source | ✅ | ❌ |
| Security model | Zero-knowledge | Zero-knowledge + Secret Key |
| Best for | Everyone, especially free users | Teams, power users, premium UX |

**Conclusion:** Bitwarden wins on price and openness. 1Password wins on UX polish and Secret Key architecture. Both are excellent choices — the right one depends on your budget and needs.

## When to Choose Each Manager

- **Bitwarden:** You want the best free option or best value paid option
- **1Password:** You want the best premium UX and have a team/family to share with
- **KeePass:** You want zero cloud dependency and are comfortable with technical setup
- **Proton Pass:** You are already in the Proton ecosystem (ProtonMail, ProtonVPN)
- **NordPass:** You are already subscribed to NordVPN and want bundled security tools
