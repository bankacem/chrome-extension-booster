<h3>Types of Trackers</h3>

<p>There are several types of trackers that can be used to collect your data. These include:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Cookies: Small text files stored on your device to track your browsing history and preferences.</li>
  <li>Pixels: Tiny images embedded in web pages to track your online activities.</li>
  <li>Fingerprinting scripts: Code used to collect information about your device's hardware and software configurations.</li>
  <li>Beacons: Small files used to track your interactions with web pages and applications.</li>
</ul>

<h3>How Trackers Work</h3>

<p>Trackers work by collecting data about your online activities and transmitting it to third-party servers. This data can then be used to create detailed profiles about your interests, preferences, and behaviors. By using a <strong>Chrome extension to detect trackers</strong>, you can prevent this data from being collected and transmitted, ensuring a more private and secure browsing experience.</p>
