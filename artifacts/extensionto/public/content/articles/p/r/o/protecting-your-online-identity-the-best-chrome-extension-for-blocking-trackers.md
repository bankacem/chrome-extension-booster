<h3>Types of Trackers</h3>

<p>There are several types of trackers that can be used to collect your data, including:</p>

  <li>Scripts: pieces of code that are used to collect data about your online activities</li>
  <li>Pixels: small images that are used to track your location and browsing habits</li>
</ul>
