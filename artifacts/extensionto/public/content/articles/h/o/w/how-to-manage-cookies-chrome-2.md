# How to Manage Cookies in Chrome (2026 Guide)

**Quick Answer:** Manage cookies in Chrome at **Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies**. Block third-party cookies for better privacy, allow cookies site by site, or auto-delete them on browser close.

## What Are Cookies? {#what}

Cookies are small text files websites store in your browser to remember information about you. There are two types:

- **First-party cookies:** Set by the site you're visiting. Used for login sessions, shopping carts, preferences. Generally necessary for sites to work.
- **Third-party cookies:** Set by advertisers and trackers embedded in the page. Used to track you across multiple sites and show targeted ads.

Managing cookies means keeping first-party cookies (useful) while limiting third-party cookies (invasive).

---

## Block Third-Party Cookies in Chrome {#block}

1. Chrome → **Settings → Privacy and security**
2. Click **"Third-party cookies"**
3. Select **"Block third-party cookies"**

This stops ad networks from tracking you across sites without breaking most website functionality.

---

## View Cookies on a Specific Site {#view}

1. Visit any website
2. Click the **lock icon** in the address bar
3. Click **"Cookies and site data"**
4. You'll see exactly how many cookies are stored and by which domains

Or via DevTools:
1. Press **F12**
2. Go to **Application** tab
3. Click **Cookies** in the left sidebar
4. Select the site to see all cookie names, values, and expiry dates

---

## Delete Cookies for a Specific Site {#delete-site}

1. Click the **lock icon** in the address bar
2. Click **"Cookies and site data"**
3. Click **"Manage cookies and site data"**
4. Find the site and click the trash icon

Or:
1. Chrome Settings → **Privacy and security → Delete browsing data**
2. Under "Advanced," check Cookies
3. Change time range to "All time"
4. Click **Delete data**

---

## Auto-Delete Cookies When Chrome Closes {#auto}

1. Chrome Settings → **Privacy and security → Site Settings**
2. Scroll to **"Content"** → click **"On-device site data"**
3. Select **"Delete data sites have saved to your device when you close all windows"**

This clears cookies automatically each time you close Chrome — great for shared computers.

---

## Allow Cookies for Specific Sites {#allow}

If you've blocked all cookies but need a specific site to work:

1. Chrome Settings → Site Settings → **Cookies and site data**
2. Under "Sites allowed to use third-party cookies," click **Add**
3. Enter the site URL
4. Click **Add**

## FAQ {#faq}

**Will blocking third-party cookies break websites?**
Usually not. Third-party cookies are mainly used for advertising and tracking. Occasional login flows tied to third-party auth providers may be affected — whitelist those sites if needed.

**Do cookies store passwords?**
No. Cookies store session tokens (a random string that identifies your logged-in session). Your actual password is not stored in cookies.

**What's the difference between clearing cookies and clearing cache?**
Cookies store session data (logins, preferences). Cache stores page resources (images, scripts) to make sites load faster. Clearing cookies logs you out of sites. Clearing cache just forces a fresh page load.

**Does incognito mode block cookies?**
Incognito doesn't block cookies during the session — sites can still set cookies and track you within that session. But all incognito cookies are deleted when you close the window.
