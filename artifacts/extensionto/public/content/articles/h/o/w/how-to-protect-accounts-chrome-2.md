# How to Protect Your Accounts in Chrome (2026)

**Quick Answer:** Protect your accounts in Chrome by enabling Enhanced Safe Browsing, using a password manager, enabling 2FA on every account, and installing uBlock Origin to block phishing sites and trackers.

## Enable Chrome's Enhanced Safe Browsing {#safe-browsing}

Chrome's Safe Browsing has three levels. Enhanced mode offers the strongest protection.

1. Open Chrome → **Settings**
2. Click **Privacy and security**
3. Click **Security**
4. Select **Enhanced protection**

Enhanced mode checks URLs against Google's real-time database of phishing and malware sites — not just a locally cached list.

## Use a Password Manager {#passwords}

Weak and reused passwords are the #1 cause of account takeovers. A password manager solves this by generating and storing unique, strong passwords for every site.

**Recommended:** Bitwarden (free, open source)

- Generates strong random passwords automatically
- Autofills credentials so you're never tempted to reuse passwords
- Alerts you when a site you use has been breached

---

## Enable Two-Factor Authentication {#2fa}

2FA means even if someone steals your password, they can't log in without your second factor (usually a time-based code from an app).

**Best 2FA apps for Chrome users:**
- **Authenticator** Chrome extension — generates 2FA codes directly in your browser
- **Google Authenticator** (mobile)
- **Authy** (mobile, with backup)

**Accounts to prioritize for 2FA:**
1. Your Google/Gmail account
2. Your email provider
3. Banking and financial accounts
4. Your password manager vault
5. Social media accounts

## Check for Compromised Passwords {#breach}

**Via Chrome:**
1. Go to `chrome://password-manager/checkup`
2. Chrome runs a breach check and shows compromised, reused, and weak passwords
3. Click any flagged entry to change the password immediately

**Via haveibeenpwned.com:**
- Enter your email to see if it appears in known data breaches
- Sign up for alerts for future breaches

## Block Phishing with Extensions {#phishing}

**uBlock Origin** blocks known phishing domains before the page even loads. Its filter lists include phishing site databases that update daily.

**Netcraft Extension** is specifically designed to detect phishing and fraudulent sites. It shows a risk rating for every site you visit.

## Secure Your Google Account {#google}

Since Chrome is tied to your Google account, securing Google secures Chrome.

1. Go to [myaccount.google.com/security](https://myaccount.google.com/security)
2. Enable **2-Step Verification**
3. Review **Third-party app access** — revoke anything you don't recognize
4. Check **Recent security activity** for unknown sign-ins
5. Set up a **recovery email and phone number**

## FAQ {#faq}

**Can Chrome extensions steal my account credentials?**
A malicious extension with broad permissions could attempt this. Only install extensions from publishers you trust with high user counts and regular updates.

**Is Enhanced Safe Browsing private?**
Enhanced mode sends some browsing data to Google for real-time checks. If that concerns you, use Standard protection instead.

**How do I know if I've been hacked?**
Signs include: password reset emails you didn't request, unknown login notifications, or unfamiliar devices in your Google account security page.

**Does using incognito mode protect my accounts?**
Incognito prevents Chrome from saving your history and cookies locally but does not make you anonymous to websites or protect against phishing.
