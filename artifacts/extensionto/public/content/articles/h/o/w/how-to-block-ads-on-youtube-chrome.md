# How to Block Ads on YouTube in Chrome (2026)

**Quick Answer:** The most effective way to block YouTube ads in Chrome in 2026 is **uBlock Origin** with the uAssets filter list enabled. It bypasses YouTube's anti-adblock detection and blocks pre-roll, mid-roll, and banner ads.

## Why YouTube Ads Are Hard to Block in 2026 {#why}

YouTube has significantly upgraded its anti-adblock technology since 2023. It now detects many ad blockers and shows a warning asking you to disable your blocker or subscribe to YouTube Premium.

However, **uBlock Origin** continues to stay ahead of these detections with rapid filter updates. If you see an anti-adblock warning with uBlock installed, a filter update usually fixes it within 24–48 hours.

---

## Method 1: uBlock Origin (Best) {#ublock}

uBlock Origin is the most effective and privacy-respecting ad blocker for Chrome. It's free, open source, and maintained by an active community.

**Install steps:**

1. Go to the [Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore)
2. Search **"uBlock Origin"** — look for the extension by **Raymond Hill**
3. Click **"Add to Chrome"** → **"Add extension"**
4. Open YouTube — ads should be blocked immediately

**If you see a YouTube anti-adblock warning:**

1. Click the uBlock Origin icon in your toolbar
2. Click the **gear icon** (Open the dashboard)
3. Go to **Filter lists** tab
4. Click **"Update now"** to refresh all filters
5. Reload YouTube

> **Important:** There is a fake "uBlock Origin" extension in the store. Always install from the official listing by Raymond Hill with 40M+ users.

## Method 2: AdGuard AdBlocker {#adguard}

AdGuard is another trusted Chrome extension that blocks YouTube ads effectively.

1. Search **"AdGuard AdBlocker"** in the Chrome Web Store
2. Install and open YouTube
3. If ads appear, go to AdGuard settings and enable **"YouTube ads"** filter

AdGuard also blocks trackers and has a more user-friendly interface than uBlock Origin.

---

## Method 3: SponsorBlock (Skip Sponsor Segments) {#sponsor}

SponsorBlock doesn't block ads — it skips **sponsored segments inside videos** (when a creator promotes a product mid-video). It works alongside uBlock Origin.

1. Install **SponsorBlock** from the Chrome Web Store
2. It automatically detects and skips community-labeled sponsor segments
3. You can also report new sponsor segments to help the community

Categories it skips: sponsor messages, self-promotion, intros, outros, interaction reminders.

---

## Method 4: YouTube Premium {#premium}

If you want an ad-free experience without extensions, YouTube Premium removes all ads across all devices (including mobile where extensions don't work) for a monthly fee.

This is the only official ad-free option for YouTube on Android and iOS.

## FAQ {#faq}

**Does blocking YouTube ads hurt creators?**
Yes, technically. If this matters to you, consider using YouTube Premium (creators get a share) or supporting creators directly via Patreon or channel memberships.

**Why does YouTube keep detecting my ad blocker?**
YouTube updates its detection regularly. Update your uBlock Origin filter lists and the issue usually resolves within a day or two.

**Can I block YouTube ads on my phone?**
Not with Chrome for Android. Use **Kiwi Browser** with uBlock Origin installed, or subscribe to YouTube Premium.

**Is it legal to block ads on YouTube?**
Ad blocking is legal in most countries. YouTube's terms of service discourage it, but there are no legal consequences for users.
