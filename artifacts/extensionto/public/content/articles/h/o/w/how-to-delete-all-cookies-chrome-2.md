# How to Delete All Cookies in Chrome (2026)

**Quick Answer:** Press **Ctrl+Shift+Delete** in Chrome, check "Cookies and other site data," select "All time," and click "Delete data." This clears all cookies instantly.

## Delete All Cookies (Fastest Method) {#fastest}

1. Press **Ctrl+Shift+Delete** (Windows) or **Cmd+Shift+Delete** (Mac)
2. Set time range to **"All time"**
3. Check **"Cookies and other site data"**
4. Uncheck anything you want to keep (history, cache, etc.)
5. Click **"Delete data"**

Done. All cookies across all sites are now cleared.

---

## Delete Cookies for One Specific Site {#one-site}

Don't want to log out of everything — just clear one site?

**Method 1 — Address bar:**
1. Visit the site
2. Click the **lock icon** in the address bar
3. Click **"Cookies and site data"**
4. Click **"Manage cookies and site data"**
5. Click the trash icon next to the site

**Method 2 — Site settings:**
1. Chrome Settings → **Privacy and security → Site Settings**
2. Click **"View permissions and data stored across sites"**
3. Search for the site
4. Click the site → Click **"Delete data"**

## Auto-Delete Cookies on Chrome Close {#auto}

1. Chrome Settings → **Privacy and security → Site Settings**
2. Click **"On-device site data"**
3. Select **"Delete data sites have saved to your device when you close all windows"**

Every time you close Chrome completely, all cookies are wiped. You'll need to log back in to every site each session.

---

## Delete Cookies via Chrome Settings {#settings}

1. Chrome → **Settings → Privacy and security**
2. Click **"Delete browsing data"**
3. Go to the **"Advanced"** tab for more options
4. Check **"Cookies and other site data"**
5. Set time range and click **"Delete data"**

## What Happens When You Delete Cookies {#effects}

| Effect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Logged out of sites | You'll need to sign in again everywhere |
| Preferences reset | Theme choices, language settings, etc. |
| Shopping carts cleared | Items in cart disappear |
| Ad tracking reset | Targeted ads reset temporarily |
| Site data cleared | Any locally stored site preferences gone |

**Not affected:** Bookmarks, saved passwords (stored separately), browser history (unless you checked that box too), downloaded files.

## FAQ {#faq}

**Will deleting cookies delete my saved passwords?**
No. Chrome stores passwords separately from cookies. Deleting cookies only removes session tokens and site preferences.

**How often should I clear cookies?**
For most users, clearing cookies every 1–3 months is sufficient. Privacy-conscious users may prefer monthly or automatic clearing.

**Does deleting cookies speed up Chrome?**
Marginally. A very large cookie store can slow certain operations, but the difference is usually unnoticeable. Clearing the cache has a more noticeable speed effect.

**Can websites detect if I cleared my cookies?**
Not directly. But if you were previously identified via cookie, the site will treat you as a new visitor.
