<h2 id="what-is-a-popup-blocker">What is a Popup Blocker?</h2>

<p>A popup blocker is a software program or browser extension that blocks unwanted popups and ads from appearing on your screen. These blockers can be configured to allow certain types of popups, such as those from trusted websites, while blocking others. Popup blockers are essential for anyone who wants to avoid distractions, reduce clutter, and enhance their overall browsing experience.</p>
