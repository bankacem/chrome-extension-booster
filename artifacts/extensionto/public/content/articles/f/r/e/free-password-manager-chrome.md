## What Makes a Password Manager Truly Free?

A genuinely free password manager for Chrome must offer:
- ✅ Unlimited password storage (no 20-password cap)
- ✅ Chrome extension with full autofill on the free plan
- ✅ Works across at least one device without paying
- ✅ No artificially broken autofill on the free plan
- ✅ No trial period that expires

## Top 4 Truly Free Password Managers for Chrome

### 1. Bitwarden Free — Best Free Password Manager Available

Bitwarden's free plan includes:
- Unlimited passwords (no cap)
- Unlimited devices (no restriction)
- Chrome extension with complete autofill
- Secure notes and card storage
- Basic two-factor authentication
- Password generator

This is the strongest free tier in the category by a significant margin. There is no functional limitation on password storage or autofill. The only features behind the $10/year premium: TOTP code storage in Bitwarden, encrypted file attachments, and emergency access — none of which affect basic password management.

### 2. KeePass — 100% Free, Local Storage Forever

KeePass is completely free with no paid tier, no exceptions. Your passwords stay on your device in an encrypted `.kdbx` file. Use the **KeePassXC-Browser** extension for Chrome autofill.

**Trade-off:** More setup required than cloud managers. You manage your own backup and sync.

### 3. NordPass Free (1 Active Device)

NordPass free gives you unlimited passwords on any device, but only one device can be active (logged in) at a time. Switching devices requires logging out of one first.

**Good for:** Users who primarily use one computer and want easy setup.

### 4. Proton Pass Free

From the makers of ProtonMail. The free plan includes:
- Unlimited passwords
- Chrome extension with autofill
- Built-in email alias generation (hide-my-email style)
- No device limit

The email alias feature is unique — it generates throwaway email addresses for signups so your real email is never exposed in data breaches.

## FAQ

**Q: Is Bitwarden free forever or does the free plan expire?**
Bitwarden's free plan is permanent — no trial period, no expiration. The organization has explicitly committed to always offering a free tier.

**Q: Can I use Bitwarden on my phone and computer for free?**
Yes — Bitwarden's free plan has no device limit. You can use it on Chrome on your laptop, the mobile app on your phone, and any other browser simultaneously.

**Q: What happens to my KeePass passwords if my computer breaks?**
They are lost unless you have a backup. Always back up your `.kdbx` file to an external drive or encrypted cloud storage (the file itself is encrypted, so storing it in Dropbox is safe).
