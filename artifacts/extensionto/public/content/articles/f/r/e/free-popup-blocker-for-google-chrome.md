## What Makes a Popup Blocker Truly Free?

A genuinely free popup blocker must:
- Block popups without a subscription or trial
- Not limit the number of blocked popups
- Not inject its own ads as "monetization" replacement
- Be open-source or have a clear, auditable privacy policy

## The 4 Truly Free Popup Blockers for Chrome

### 1. uBlock Origin (Open Source, 100% Free Forever)

uBlock Origin has no paid tier. Every feature — popup blocking, ad blocking, tracker blocking, custom filters — is free and always will be. The project is maintained by volunteers and funded by donations only. No data is collected, no ads are served.

**Install:** [Chrome Web Store — uBlock Origin](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ublock-origin/cjpalhdlnbpafiamejdnhcphjbkeiagm)

### 2. Popup Blocker (Strict) — Open Source

Free, open-source, and single-purpose. Intercepts `window.open()` calls and shows you what was blocked before you decide to allow or deny. No ads, no premium plan, no telemetry.

### 3. Chrome's Built-In Popup Blocker (No Extension Needed)

Chrome has a native popup blocker enabled by default. For most users, enabling it plus Chrome's "Additional content settings" covers 95% of popup cases — for free, with no extension needed at all.

**Enable it:** `chrome://settings/content/popups` → **Blocked (recommended)**

### 4. Stands Free AdBlocker

Stands is free for popup and ad blocking. Unlike Poper Blocker (which has a premium tier that limits some features), Stands' core popup-blocking functionality is not paywalled.

## Free vs. Premium: What Do You Actually Lose?

Most popup blockers lock these features behind paid tiers:
- Cross-device sync of whitelist settings
- Advanced reporting/statistics dashboards
- Priority customer support
- Extra filter lists

**None of these affect popup blocking itself.** The free tiers above block popups just as effectively as any premium plan. The paid tier is about convenience, not protection quality.

## FAQ

**Q: Is uBlock Origin really free forever?**
Yes. Raymond Hill (gorhill), the developer, has explicitly stated uBlock Origin will never have a paid tier. It's funded entirely by voluntary donations. The full source code is on GitHub.

**Q: Does the free Chrome built-in popup blocker work on aggressive sites?**
It handles basic `window.open()` popups well. It struggles with click-triggered pop-unders on streaming/torrent sites. For those, add uBlock Origin.

**Q: Are there any hidden costs in "free" popup blockers?**
The hidden cost in some free blockers is your data. Extensions from unknown developers may sell browsing history. Stick to open-source tools (uBlock Origin, Popup Blocker Strict) or audited products from reputable companies.
