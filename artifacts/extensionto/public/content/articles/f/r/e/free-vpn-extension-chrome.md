## The Critical Warning About Free VPNs

Before choosing a free VPN extension, understand the business model: if you are not paying, the product is often your browsing data. Many free VPN extensions:

- Log and sell your browsing history to advertisers
- Inject tracking cookies into your browsing sessions
- Serve ads by inspecting your traffic
- Have no published or audited privacy policy

**Only install free VPN extensions from companies with:**
- A published, independently audited no-log policy
- A clear freemium business model (not data resale)
- A transparency report or warrant canary

### 1. Windscribe — Best Free VPN Extension Overall

**Free data:** 10 GB/month (15 GB with email verification)
**Free servers:** 10+ countries
**No-log policy:** ✅ Audited
**WebRTC leak protection:** ✅ Built in
**Ad/tracker blocking:** ✅ R.O.B.E.R.T. DNS blocker included free
**Business model:** Freemium — paid tier funds the free tier, no data resale

Windscribe is the strongest free VPN extension available. The built-in R.O.B.E.R.T. system blocks ads, trackers, and malware domains at DNS level — adding popup blocking on top of VPN protection. 10 GB covers typical daily browsing but not heavy video streaming.

### 2. ProtonVPN Free — Best for Unlimited Data

**Free data:** ✅ Unlimited — no data cap
**Free servers:** 3 countries (US, Netherlands, Japan)
**No-log policy:** ✅ Independently audited
**WebRTC leak protection:** ✅
**Speed:** Slower than paid (free servers congested during peak hours)
**Jurisdiction:** Switzerland — strong privacy laws

ProtonVPN is the only major VPN offering an unlimited free tier. The limitation is server selection and speed. Proton's privacy track record (ProtonMail, ProtonDrive) is the strongest of any VPN company.

---

### 3. Cloudflare 1.1.1.1 WARP — Best for Speed, Not Full Anonymity

**Free data:** ✅ Unlimited
**Privacy model:** Hides IP from websites; Cloudflare can still see your traffic
**Speed:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Near-zero latency impact
**WebRTC leak protection:** Partial
**Best for:** Hiding IP from ISP; NOT for anonymity from all parties

Use WARP if you want a fast, free connection upgrade and trust Cloudflare. Do not use WARP if you need anonymity from all third parties.

### 4. TunnelBear Free — Best for Occasional Use

**Free data:** 2 GB/month
**No-log policy:** ✅ Annually audited and publicly published
**WebRTC leak protection:** ✅
**Best for:** Occasional public Wi-Fi protection

TunnelBear's 2 GB/month limit is restrictive for daily use, but its annual published security audit makes it one of the most transparently-vetted free VPNs available.

## WebRTC Leak Test (Required Step)

After installing any VPN extension:
1. Connect the VPN
2. Visit `browserleaks.com/webrtc`
3. Check if your real IP appears under any IP address field

If your real IP is visible → the VPN has a WebRTC leak and is not fully protecting you.

## FAQ

**Q: Will a free VPN extension slow down my browsing?**
Windscribe and Cloudflare WARP have minimal speed impact. ProtonVPN free can be noticeably slower during peak hours due to server congestion.

**Q: Is 10 GB of Windscribe enough for daily use?**
For text browsing and email: yes. For HD video streaming: 10 GB lasts roughly 3–5 hours of video.

**Q: Can I use a free VPN extension for Netflix?**
Most free VPN servers are quickly blocked by Netflix. For reliable streaming access, a paid VPN (NordVPN, Surfshark) is required.
