<h2 id="installing-extensions">Installing Extensions on Chrome Android</h2>

<p>Installing an extension to Chrome Android is a straightforward process. Here's a step-by-step guide:</p>

<ol>
    <li>Open the Google Chrome browser on your Android device.</li>
    <li>Tap the three dots in the top-right corner of the screen.</li>
    <li>Tap "Settings" from the drop-down menu.</li>
    <li>Scroll down to the "Advanced" section.</li>
    <li>Tap "Extensions" to access the extensions menu.</li>
    <li>Tap "Chrome Web Store" to browse available extensions.</li>
    <li>Search for the extension you want to install and tap "Add to Chrome" to install it.</li>
</ol>

<p>Some popular extensions that you can install on your Chrome Android browser include <a href="/extension/quick-screenshot-lite" class="text-primary font-medium hover:underline">Quick Screenshot Lite</a> for capturing screenshots and <a href="/extension/auto-dark-mode-switcher" class="text-primary font-medium hover:underline">Auto Dark Mode Switcher</a> for <a href="/blog/how-to-hibernate-inactive-tabs-automatically-6" class="internal-link" title="How to Hibernate Inactive Tabs Automatically: The Ultimate Guide to a Faster Browser">automatically</a> switching between dark and light modes.</p>
