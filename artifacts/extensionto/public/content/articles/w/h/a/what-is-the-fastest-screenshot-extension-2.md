# What Is the Fastest Screenshot Extension for Chrome? (2026)

**Quick Answer:** For pure capture speed, **Chrome DevTools** (built-in, no extension) wins. Among extensions, **GoFullPage** is fastest for full-page captures and **Lightshot** is fastest for visible-area and region captures.

## Chrome DevTools — Fastest Overall {#devtools}

No extension to install. No toolbar icon to click.

**Workflow:**
- F12 → Ctrl+Shift+P → "screenshot" → Enter
- Keyboard-only workflow: under 3 seconds start to finish

**Limitation:** No annotation. Download only (no clipboard copy option natively). Requires knowing the shortcut.

---

## GoFullPage — Fastest Full-Page Extension {#gofullpage}

GoFullPage captures full pages faster than any other extension because it uses a direct DOM rendering approach rather than stitching scrolled screenshots.

**Workflow:**
- Click icon → automatic capture → PNG ready in ~1.5 seconds

**Best for:** Developers, designers capturing entire page layouts.

---

## Lightshot — Fastest Visible-Area Extension {#lightshot}

Lightshot activates with a single keyboard shortcut (PrtScn key) and lets you drag to select a region. It's the fastest for region captures.

**Workflow:**
- Press PrtScn → drag to select → press Enter to save

Lightshot copies to clipboard AND saves as file simultaneously.

**Note:** Lightshot uploads screenshots to lightshot.net servers if you use the share feature. For privacy-sensitive captures, save locally only.

---

## Awesome Screenshot — Best Balance {#awesome}

Not the fastest, but the most versatile. Captures visible area, full page, and region — plus annotation, blur, and direct upload.

Best for users who need all three capture types plus annotation without switching tools.

## FAQ {#faq}

**What's the keyboard shortcut for the fastest screenshot?**
For Chrome DevTools: F12, then Ctrl+Shift+P, then type "screen" and press Enter. For Lightshot: just press PrtScn.

**Which screenshot extension works with Chrome's incognito mode?**
Most extensions don't work in incognito by default. To enable: `chrome://extensions` → click the extension → toggle "Allow in Incognito."

**Can I set a custom keyboard shortcut for screenshot extensions?**
Yes. Go to `chrome://extensions/shortcuts` to assign custom keyboard shortcuts to any extension's action.
