## Ad Blocking (MV3 Updated)

### 1. uBlock Origin Lite
- **MV3:** ✅ Yes (official MV3 rebuild of uBlock Origin)
- **Developer:** Raymond Hill (same as original)
- **Blocks:** Ads, trackers, malware domains, pop-ups
- **Doesn't block:** Server-side injected YouTube/Spotify ads (SSAI)
- **RAM:** ~18MB idle
- **Data collection:** None
- **Best for:** Chrome users who want free, ethical, zero-data-collection ad blocking
- **Install:** Search "uBlock Origin Lite" in Chrome Web Store, verify developer is Raymond Hill

### 2. AdGuard AdBlocker
- **MV3:** ✅ Yes
- **Developer:** Adguard Software Ltd
- **Blocks:** Ads, trackers, phishing domains, fingerprinting (Stealth Mode)
- **Unique feature:** Stealth Mode hides your search queries and removes tracking URL parameters
- **RAM:** ~42MB idle
- **YouTube:** More reliable than uBlock Lite for YouTube in 2026
- **Best for:** Users who want stronger tracker protection alongside ad blocking

### 3. Blockify
- **MV3:** ✅ Yes
- **Speciality:** Audio and video streaming ads (YouTube, Spotify, Twitch, Hulu)
- **Method:** Detects SSAI ad segments and skips or mutes — doesn't try to block the request
- **RAM:** ~35MB idle
- **Best for:** Heavy streaming users frustrated with YouTube ads slipping through

> **Note:** Do not run two ad blockers simultaneously. Choose one based on your primary use case.

## Privacy

### 4. ClearURLs
- **Developer:** Kevin Röbert (open source)
- **What it does:** Removes tracking parameters from URLs silently (utm_source, fbclid, etc.)
- **RAM:** ~8MB idle
- **Data collection:** None — runs 100% locally
- **Best for:** Anyone who clicks links from emails, social media, newsletters

### 5. Cookie AutoDelete
- **What it does:** Deletes cookies from closed tabs automatically; keeps whitelisted site cookies
- **RAM:** ~20MB idle
- **Setup:** Whitelist sites you want to stay logged into, enable auto-clean
- **Best for:** Privacy-conscious users who want cookie cleanup without manual effort

### 6. Privacy Badger
- **Developer:** Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF) — non-profit, open source
- **How it's different:** Learns which trackers follow you across sites and blocks them, even unknown ones not on block lists
- **RAM:** ~22MB idle
- **Best paired with:** uBlock Origin Lite (complementary, not conflicting)

## Passwords

### 7. Bitwarden
- **Price:** Free / $10/year Premium
- **Encryption:** AES-256, zero-knowledge (Bitwarden cannot read your vault)
- **Open source:** ✅ Audited by Cure53 (2022, 2023)
- **Breach history:** None
- **RAM:** ~25MB idle
- **Best for:** Everyone — the default recommendation

### 8. 1Password
- **Price:** $2.99/month
- **Unique feature:** Secret Key system — vault requires both master password AND a device-specific key. Protects against server-side breaches.
- **Best for:** Families, teams needing collaborative password management

## Tab Management

### 9. OneTab
- **RAM savings in test:** 94% reduction (22 tabs → 195MB from 3.1GB)
- **RAM:** ~12MB idle
- **Best for:** Anyone with chronic tab overload

### 10. Workona
- **What it does:** Creates named workspaces (sets of tabs) that persist across browser restarts
- **Free plan:** 5 workspaces
- **RAM:** ~30MB idle
- **Best for:** People who work on multiple projects simultaneously

### 11. TabGroup Vault
- **Gap it fills:** Chrome's native tab groups don't save across restarts; TabGroup Vault does
- **Price:** Free (5 snapshots) / $29 lifetime
- **Best for:** Chrome tab group power users

### 12. Auto Tab Discard
- **What it does:** Suspends inactive tabs to free RAM
- **Different from Memory Saver:** More control over timing and whitelist
- **RAM:** ~15MB idle

## Writing

## FAQ

**Which extensions from this list work on Firefox?**
All non-ad-blocker extensions have Firefox versions (or work via the Firefox Add-ons store). Full uBlock Origin (not Lite) is available on Firefox and is superior to any Chrome version.

**Can I run all 30 at once?**
Technically yes, but the RAM total would exceed 800MB. Install only what you actively use. 5-8 extensions is the practical limit for most users.

**Are all these extensions free?**
Most have free tiers. Paid options: 1Password ($2.99/month), Workona (paid plan for more workspaces), TabGroup Vault ($29 lifetime for Pro).
