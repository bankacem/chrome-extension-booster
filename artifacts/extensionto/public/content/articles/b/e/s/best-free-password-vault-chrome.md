# Best Free Password Vault for Chrome (2026)

A password vault securely stores and organizes your credentials. These are the best options that are completely free — not trial versions, not limited tiers with paywalled autofill.

## What to Look for in a Free Password Vault

- ✅ AES-256 encryption minimum
- ✅ Zero-knowledge architecture (the company cannot see your passwords)
- ✅ Unlimited password storage on free plan
- ✅ Chrome autofill on free plan
- ✅ Independent security audit

---

## Best Free Password Vaults for Chrome (2026)

### 1. Bitwarden — Best Free Vault Overall

**Encryption:** AES-256-CBC + PBKDF2-SHA256 / Argon2id (2024+)
**Zero-knowledge:** ✅ — Bitwarden's servers never see your plaintext passwords
**Free plan:** Unlimited passwords, unlimited devices, full autofill, secure notes
**Open source:** ✅ — auditable by anyone
**Audit:** Independently audited by Cure53

Bitwarden's free vault is the most capable genuinely free option available. The encryption key is derived from your master password on your device before anything is sent to Bitwarden's servers.

### 2. KeePass — Local Vault, Free Forever

**Encryption:** AES-256 or ChaCha20
**Zero-knowledge:** ✅ — the vault never leaves your device
**Free plan:** Everything included, permanently free
**Open source:** ✅

Your vault is a `.kdbx` file on your device. Nothing is stored on any external server. The KeePassXC-Browser extension connects Chrome directly to your local KeePassXC application for autofill.

### 3. Proton Pass — Best Free Vault with Email Aliases

**Encryption:** AES-256 with end-to-end encryption
**Zero-knowledge:** ✅
**Free plan:** Unlimited passwords, unlimited devices, email alias generation
**Open source:** ✅ (client-side code)

Proton Pass is unique — the free plan includes email alias generation. Every signup can use a different throwaway address so your real email is never exposed in breaches.

## FAQ

**Q: Are free password vaults as secure as paid ones?**
Yes — the encryption and zero-knowledge architecture in Bitwarden Free, KeePass, and Proton Pass Free are identical to any paid tier. Payment does not buy stronger encryption.

**Q: What is the safest free password vault?**
KeePass is the safest in the sense that your data never leaves your device. Bitwarden is the safest cloud-based free vault with the most thorough independent audits.
