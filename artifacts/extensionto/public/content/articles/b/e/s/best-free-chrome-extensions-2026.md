# Best Free Chrome Extensions 2026 — 20 Extensions, Zero Cost

"Free" means different things to different extensions. Some call themselves free while locking core features behind a subscription. Some are free because they monetize your data. This list only includes extensions where the core daily functionality is genuinely free — no credit card required, no essential feature paywalled, no data sold to fund it.

---

## Our Definition of Truly Free

To be on this list, an extension must:
- Provide its core, useful function at zero cost with no account required (or with a free account)
- Not require payment to do what it's advertised to do
- Not monetize through data sales (we checked privacy policies)

Extensions that are free for basic use but heavily push paywalls for anything useful are not on this list.

## The 20 Best Genuinely Free Chrome Extensions

### Productivity and Workflow

**1. OneTab**
Collapses all open tabs into a list, recovering up to 95% of the RAM those tabs consumed. Completely free, no premium tier, no account needed.
- RAM: 12MB | Users: 2M+

**2. StayFocusd**
Blocks distracting sites after a daily time budget runs out. The Nuclear Option blocks everything for a set period with zero override capability. Entirely free, no account required.
- RAM: 10MB | Users: 900K+

**3. Momentum**
Replaces new tab page with a focus goal, weather, and to-do list. Free tier is genuinely useful. Premium adds more background photos and integrations, but the free version covers the core use case.
- RAM: 20MB | Users: 3M+

**4. Todoist (Basic)**
Right-click any page to capture it as a task. Free plan supports up to 5 projects, which covers most users' needs. The capture-from-browser feature is fully free.
- RAM: 20MB

---

### Ad Blocking and Privacy

**5. uBlock Origin Lite**
The free, open-source, MV3-compatible ad blocker for Chrome. No account, no data collection, no acceptable ads program. Developer: Raymond Hill, same as original uBlock Origin.
- RAM: 18MB | Users: 2M+

**6. ClearURLs**
Silently removes tracking parameters from every link you click. Completely free, open source, no account, no server communication. Just installs and works.
- RAM: 8MB | Users: 900K+

**7. Cookie AutoDelete**
Automatically deletes cookies from closed tabs while preserving whitelisted sites. Free, open source, no data collection.
- RAM: 20MB | Users: 700K+

**8. Privacy Badger**
Made by the EFF (non-profit). Learns and blocks behavioral trackers automatically. Free forever — EFF is funded by donations, not data sales.
- RAM: 22MB

---

### Password Management

**9. Bitwarden**
End-to-end encrypted password manager. The free individual plan includes: unlimited passwords, unlimited devices, secure notes, password generator, and breach alerts. This is not a stripped demo — it's a fully functional password manager that costs nothing.
- RAM: 25MB | Users: 5M+

---

### Dark Mode and Reading

**10. Dark Reader**
Full dark mode for every website. The free version has no feature limits — all the brightness/contrast/temperature controls are available at no cost. There is a voluntary "Support Dark Reader" donation button, not a paywall.
- RAM: 30MB | Users: 5M+

**11. Mercury Reader (Readability)**
Strips articles down to clean, ad-free, readable text. Completely free, no account.
- RAM: 15MB

---

### Screenshots

**12. GoFullPage (Free Tier)**
Full-page screenshots as PNG or PDF. The free tier covers everything most users need. Premium adds editing tools.
- RAM: 25MB | Users: 2M+

**13. Chrome DevTools Screenshot (Built-in)**
Not an extension — it's built into Chrome. Press F12, then Ctrl+Shift+P, type "screenshot." Full-page, visible area, region, or element — all free, all built-in.
- RAM: 0MB extra

**14. Lightshot**
Region and visible-area screenshots with instant shareable links. Entirely free, no account needed for basic use.
- RAM: 15MB

### Writing

Grammar and spell checker supporting 30+ languages. The free tier catches grammar errors, style issues, and spelling mistakes. Lighter than Grammarly at ~50MB vs 150MB.
- RAM: ~50MB

## FAQ

**Are any of these extensions going to start charging in the future?**
Open-source extensions funded by donations (Dark Reader, uBlock Origin Lite, ClearURLs, Cookie AutoDelete) are the safest bet for staying free. Company-backed freemium tools may change their pricing models.

**What is Bitwarden's business model if the password manager is free?**
Bitwarden makes money from Business and Enterprise plans for organizations. Individual free users subsidize nothing — the individual free plan is genuinely part of their model.

**Is a free ad blocker as good as a paid one?**
For Chrome in 2026: yes. uBlock Origin Lite is free and outperforms most paid ad blockers in blocking efficiency per MB of RAM used.
