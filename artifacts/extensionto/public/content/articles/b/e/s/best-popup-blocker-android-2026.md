# Best Popup Blocker for Android 2026 — Ranked and Tested

We tested popup blocking across 30 Android browsers and apps using 5 popup types: new-window, overlay div, cookie consent, notification requests, and exit-intent. Here's the honest ranking.

---

## Testing Overview

**Devices tested:** Samsung Galaxy S24, Pixel 8, budget Motorola G device
**Popup types tested:** New window, overlay div, cookie banner, notification request, exit-intent
**Browsers tested:** Chrome, Firefox, Brave, Kiwi
**Period:** May 2026

---

## #1 — Firefox for Android + Full uBlock Origin (Best Overall)

**Score:** 94% across all popup types

Full uBlock Origin on Firefox is the most comprehensive popup blocker available on Android. Its Annoyances filter list removes overlay popups, cookie banners, exit-intent popups, and notification dialogs — all categories of popup that Chrome's built-in misses.

**Setup:** Play Store → Firefox → Menu → Add-ons → uBlock Origin → enable Annoyances filter

**Overlay popup blocking:** ✅ Excellent  
**Cookie banners:** ✅ Excellent  
**Exit-intent:** ✅ Good  
**New-window popups:** ✅ Excellent  
**Cost:** Free

## #2 — Brave Browser (Easiest Setup, Strong Results)

**Score:** 88% across all popup types

Brave's Shields block ad scripts that generate most popups. Its built-in setup requires zero configuration — install and browse.

**Overlay popup blocking:** ✅ Good (catches ad-script-generated overlays)  
**Cookie banners:** ⚠️ Partial  
**Exit-intent:** ⚠️ Partial  
**New-window popups:** ✅ Excellent  
**Cost:** Free

## #3 — Kiwi Browser + uBlock Origin Lite (Chrome Experience)

**Score:** 85% across all popup types

Nearly matches Firefox + uBlock, with the advantage of Chrome's familiar interface. The slight gap is due to uBlock Lite's MV3 limitations vs. full uBlock Origin.

**Setup:** Play Store → Kiwi → Extensions → uBlock Origin Lite → Annoyances filter  
**Cost:** Free

## #4 — Chrome with Built-in Settings + Private DNS

**Score:** 71% (stays in Chrome)

The only method that keeps you in the official Chrome app. Private DNS (AdGuard) blocks some popup-triggering ad networks. Chrome's built-in setting blocks new-window popups.

**Overlay popup blocking:** ❌ (not effective)  
**New-window popups:** ✅  
**Notification dialogs:** ✅  
**Cost:** Free

## FAQ

**What's the difference between popup types on mobile vs desktop?**
Mobile popups are predominantly overlay divs (full-screen signup walls) and notification requests. New-window popups are rarer on mobile because mobile browsers handle them differently. This is why Chrome's built-in blocker (new windows) is less effective on mobile than desktop.

**Can I block popups in Android apps (not just browsers)?**
For app-level popup blocking (ads in apps), use Private DNS (AdGuard DNS) or AdGuard for Android app. Browser-based solutions only affect your browser.
