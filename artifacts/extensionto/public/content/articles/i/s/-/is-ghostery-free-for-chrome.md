# Is Ghostery Free for Chrome? (2026 Answer)

**Quick Answer:** Yes, Ghostery is free for Chrome. The core ad blocking and tracker blocking features are completely free with no time limit. Ghostery also offers optional paid products (Ghostery Midnight VPN and Ghostery Private Browser), but the Chrome extension itself needs no payment.

## What Is Free in Ghostery? {#free}

The free Ghostery Chrome extension includes:

- **Ad blocking** — removes ads from most websites
- **Tracker blocking** — blocks over 5,000 known trackers
- **Tracker visibility** — shows which trackers are on each page
- **Enhanced Anti-Tracking** — anonymizes remaining tracking data
- **Never-Consent** — automatically rejects cookie consent popups
- **Basic Ghostery stats** — monthly tracker blocking summary

These features work indefinitely with no subscription required.

---

## What Is Paid in Ghostery? {#paid}

Ghostery's paid products are separate applications, not extension upgrades:

- **Ghostery Midnight** — a VPN + system-level tracker blocker (~$14/month)
- **Ghostery Private Browser** — a standalone privacy browser with built-in features

The Chrome *extension* does not have a premium tier. Everything in the extension is free.

## Is the Free Version Enough? {#enough}

For the vast majority of Chrome users, **yes**. The free Ghostery extension blocks ads and trackers as effectively as most paid alternatives. The cookie consent auto-rejection ("Never-Consent") feature alone saves significant time.

You only need paid Ghostery products if you want:
- A VPN for IP masking
- Tracker blocking in apps outside Chrome (Spotify, native apps, etc.)

## FAQ {#faq}

**Will Ghostery ever charge for the Chrome extension?**
The current ownership (Cliqz/Ghostery GmbH) has not indicated plans to paywall the extension. The business model relies on Ghostery Midnight subscriptions, not extension fees.

**Does Ghostery's free version show ads within the extension UI?**
No. Ghostery's own interface is ad-free.

**Is there a limit to how many trackers Ghostery blocks in the free version?**
No. All tracker blocking features are unlimited in the free extension.

**Do I need a Ghostery account to use the free extension?**
No. The core blocking features work without signing up. An account unlocks cross-device sync of your Ghostery settings.
