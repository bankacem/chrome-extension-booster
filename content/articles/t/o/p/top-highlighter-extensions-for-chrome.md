## The 5 Best Highlighter Extensions for Chrome

### 1. Weava Highlighter (Editor's Choice)
Weava is the industry leader for a reason. It allows you to organize your highlights into \"folders\" and \"subfolders\" directly in your browser.
*   **Best Feature:** Works on both websites and PDF files seamlessly.
*   **Pro Tip:** Use different colors for different themes (e.g., Yellow for facts, Green for quotes).

### 2. Glasp: Social Web Highlighter
Glasp is unique because it is \"social.\" You can see what other people are highlighting on the same articles, making it a great tool for discovering new insights.
*   **Best Feature:** One-click copy-paste to Notion with perfect formatting.

### 3. Hypothesis
Designed for deep collaboration. Hypothesis is used by many universities because it allows groups to annotate the same page together.
*   **Best Feature:** High-level academic privacy and open-source standards.

### 4. Liner: AI Research Copilot
Liner has integrated AI (GPT-4o) into its highlighter. When you highlight a section, you can ask the AI to \"summarize this\" or \"find related articles.\"
*   **Best Feature:** AI-powered discovery based on your highlighting habits.

### 5. SuperSimple Highlighter
If you want something lightweight that doesn't require an account or a cloud subscription, this is the one.
*   **Best Feature:** Does one thing perfectly—highlights text and remembers it when you return to the page.

## FAQ: Mastering Web Annotations

### Do highlighter extensions work on PDFs?
Most basic ones do not. If you need PDF support, **Weava** and **Hypothesis** are the best choices.

### Will my highlights disappear if I clear my cache?
Cloud-based tools like **Weava** and **Glasp** save your highlights to their servers, so they are safe. Lightweight tools like **SuperSimple Highlighter** may lose data if you delete your browser data.

### Can I share my highlights with others?
Yes! **Hypothesis** and **Glasp** have built-in sharing features. You can send a link to your \"annotated\" version of a page to anyone.

### How do I export my highlights to Notion?
Tools like **Glasp** and **Liner** have \"Direct to Notion\" integrations. For others, you can usually export to a CSV or Markdown file and import it manually.
