## Deep Dive: Why These Tools are \"Utile\"

### 1. Redirect Shield (Security)
Most users think they are safe as long as they don't click \"Allow\" on a pop-up. But \"redirect loops\" can steal your browser cookies without you ever clicking a thing. Redirect Shield intercepts these chains and keeps you on the page you intended to visit.

### 2. ProTab Suspender (Productivity)
Chrome is famous for being a memory hog. ProTab Suspender solves this by \"unloading\" tabs you haven't looked at in 15 minutes. The tab stays in your bar, but it consumes zero resources until you click it again.

### 3. Quick Screenshot Lite (Workflow)
Most screenshot tools require an account or try to sell you cloud storage. Quick Screenshot Lite is different: one click, one local file. It’s the tool I use for every guide on this site.

## FAQ: Building Your Extension Strategy

### How many extensions are \"too many\"?
I recommend keeping your active list under **12.** Beyond that, the memory overhead of the extensions themselves can start to slow down the browser.

### Do these extensions work on Microsoft Edge?
Yes! Edge is built on the same \"Chromium\" engine, so you can install any of these directly from the Chrome Web Store.

### Are these tools safe for my data?
We prioritize \"Privacy First\" tools. Extensions like **SecuraKey Pro** and **Bitwarden** use end-to-end encryption, meaning even the developers can't see your data.
