# Keyword Cannibalization Report — extensionto.com
**Audit Date:** May 13, 2026
**Status:** READ-ONLY. No changes made.

---

## Executive Summary

Keyword cannibalization is the **most severe SEO problem** on this site. With 499 articles targeting a narrow niche (Chrome browser extensions), the content library has been systematically over-produced around the same 8–10 core topics, resulting in dozens of articles competing against each other for identical or near-identical search queries. The worst affected clusters — screenshot tools (51 articles), download extensions (50 articles), and RAM/memory management (39 articles) — collectively represent 140 articles fighting over perhaps 10 distinct commercial intents.

**Estimated articles involved in severe cannibalization: 250–300 (50–60% of library)**

---

## 1. What Is Keyword Cannibalization Here

In this context, cannibalization occurs when multiple site pages target:
- The **same primary keyword** (e.g., "best screenshot extension for Chrome")
- **Synonymous intent** (e.g., "chrome screenshot addon" vs "chrome snipping tool" vs "capture screen chrome" — all point to the same user need)
- **Template variations** of the same title (e.g., 7+ articles starting "Unlock the full potential of...")

Unlike traditional SEO where cannibalization is article-by-article, this site suffers from **cluster-level cannibalization** where entire groups of 30–50 articles compete on the same topic.

## 5. Cannibalization Impact on Rankings

When Google encounters dozens of articles targeting the same query from the same domain, it must choose one to rank — typically the "best" one. The consequences of not resolving this:

1. **None of the duplicate articles rank high** — Google splits authority across all of them
2. **The extension pages may rank below the blog articles** — weakening commercial conversion
3. **Click-through rate fragments** — users see multiple results from extensionto.com and may choose a competitor instead
4. **The helpful content system flags thin/redundant content** — this can suppress the entire domain

---

## 6. Consolidation Strategy

### Approach: Hub-and-Spoke Model

For each major topic cluster, designate one **hub (pillar) article** and convert duplicates to either:
- **Spokes** (narrower sub-topic articles that link back to hub), or
- **301 redirects** to the hub

**Example for Screenshot cluster:**

```
HUB: best-chrome-screenshot-extensions-2026-complete-guide (already designated as pillar)
  ↓
SPOKES (keep as supporting articles):
  - full-page-screenshot-chrome-guide (unique angle: full-page)
  - capture-scrolling-webpages-as-png-or-pdf (unique angle: scrolling)
  - best-screenshot-extension-for-developers-and-designers (unique angle: dev use)
  
REDIRECT (301 to hub):
  - All remaining 47 screenshot articles
```
