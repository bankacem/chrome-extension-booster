<p>Both Privacy Badger and Ghostery are designed to <strong>block trackers</strong> and protect your online identity. However, they have different approaches and features that set them apart. <strong>Privacy Badger</strong> is a free, open-source browser extension developed by the Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF), while <strong>Ghostery</strong> is a proprietary tool that offers both free and paid versions.</p>

<h3>Key Features of Privacy Badger</h3>

<p><strong>Privacy Badger</strong> is known for its simplicity and effectiveness. Some of its key features include:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Automatic tracking protection: <strong>Privacy Badger</strong> blocks trackers based on their behavior, rather than relying on a pre-defined list.</li>
  <li>Learning mode: The extension learns and adapts to new trackers over time, ensuring that you're always protected.</li>
  <li>No data collection: <strong>Privacy Badger</strong> doesn't collect any personal data, making it a great option for those who value their online anonymity.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Key Features of Ghostery</h3>

<p><strong>Ghostery</strong>, on the other hand, offers a more <a href="/blog/ghostery-chrome-extension-winner" class="internal-link" title="Unlocking the Power of Online Privacy: A Comprehensive Guide to Ghostery Plugin Chrome"><a href="/blog/ublock-origin-vs-ghostery-for-chrome-android-a-comprehensive-comparison-mmb7lsoyr8n" class="internal-link" title="uBlock Origin vs Ghostery for Chrome Android: A Comprehensive Comparison">comprehensive</a></a> set of features, including:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Tracker blocking: <strong>Ghostery</strong> blocks trackers, but also provides more detailed information about the trackers it detects.</li>
  <li>Anti-tracking: The extension uses anti-tracking technology to prevent trackers from collecting your data.</li>
  <li>Ad blocking: <strong>Ghostery</strong> also blocks ads, making for a more streamlined browsing experience.</li>
</ul>
