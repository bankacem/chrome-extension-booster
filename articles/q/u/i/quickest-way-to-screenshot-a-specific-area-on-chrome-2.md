<h2 id="method2">Method 2: Using the Built-in Chrome Screenshot Tool</h2>

<p>Chrome also has a built-in screenshot tool that allows you to capture specific areas of the page. To use the built-in Chrome screenshot tool, follow these steps:</p>

<ol>
    <li>Open the Chrome browser and navigate to the webpage you want to capture.</li>
    <li>Press the <strong>Ctrl + Shift + I</strong> keys (Windows) or <strong>Cmd + Shift + I</strong> keys (Mac) to open the Developer Tools.</li>
    <li>Click on the <strong>Elements</strong> tab in the Developer Tools window.</li>
    <li>Click on the <strong>...</strong> menu in the top right corner of the Elements tab.</li>
    <li>Select <strong>Capture screenshot</strong> from the dropdown menu.</li>
    <li>Select the area of the page you want to capture by clicking and dragging the crosshairs.</li>
    <li>Release the mouse button to capture the screenshot.</li>
</ol>
