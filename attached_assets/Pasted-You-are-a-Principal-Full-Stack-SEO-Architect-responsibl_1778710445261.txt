1. SPA / No SSR

- All SEO content (meta tags, article content, canonical URLs) is injected client-side
- This severely impacts indexing and Google visibility

2. Missing SEO Metadata
- 499/499 articles are missing meta_description in the index layer
- Only Markdown files contain partial metadata

- 250–300 articles overlap in 6 major clusters:
  - screenshots
  - downloads
  - RAM/memory
  - ad-blockers
  - privacy tools
  - popup blockers

4. Broken Taxonomy
- "Screenshots & Screen Capture" category contains 43% of site content incorrectly grouped
- Topic clustering is invalid and misleading

5. AI Author Signal Issue
- 337 articles use "AI Generator" as author
- This is harmful for SEO trust signals

6. Content Quality Issues
- 40 duplicate excerpts
- 5 partial articles in sitemap
- 4 stub articles (<200 words)
- 2 duplicate/corrupted slugs

7. Crawl Waste
- Admin routes (/settings, /admin, /seo-dashboard) are publicly indexable

8. Sitemap is technically synced but semantically polluted

MISSION OBJECTIVE:

Fix the entire SEO + architecture system WITHOUT breaking production.
