<h2 id="built-in-screenshot">Built-in Screenshot Capabilities in Chrome</h2>
<p>Chrome provides a basic screenshot feature that allows users to capture parts of or the entire webpage. To access this feature, you can use the keyboard shortcut <strong>Ctrl + Shift + I</strong> (Windows) or <strong>Command + Option + I</strong> (Mac) to open the Developer Tools, then navigate to the "Elements" tab, and finally click on the "Device Toolbar" to toggle device mode, where you can find the screenshot option. However, this method can be somewhat cumbersome and limited in its functionality.</p>

<h3>Limitations of Built-in Screenshot Feature</h3>
