## Privacy, Permissions and Performance: What to Check Before You Install
Chrome extensions can read and modify pages you capture, so permissions, privacy, and performance deserve a close look before you install. Under Chrome's permission model, many screenshot tools request host access to scroll and stitch, but the best minimize scope to activeTab and scripting, process everything locally offline, and avoid mandatory accounts or cloud uploads. Be wary of tools that require broad access, inject ads, or send images to external servers without a clear retention policy and encryption.

Check the Chrome Web Store privacy practices section, the extension's privacy policy, and whether capture and editing happen on-device. Local-first tools like GoFullPage and FullPage Capture keep PNG/PDF generation in the browser, work offline, and keep your captures private. Cloud-first options enable convenient link sharing but mean your pages are stored remotely, so review data retention and sharing controls.

Performance matters too. Ultra-light extensions under 200KB typically add negligible load when idle, while heavier feature-rich tools can increase memory use and startup time. We profiled background activity with DevTools Performance: idle overhead should be near zero, and capture should not freeze the page excessively. Also review extension size, update frequency, support responsiveness, and transparent pricing. The lightest, most private tool that reliably stitches your target pages is usually the best long-term choice.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### 1. What is the best screenshot chrome extension full page in 2026 for accurate stitching?
GoFullPage and FireShot lead for stitching accuracy in 2026 testing. Both handled long pages over 10,000 pixels, sticky headers, and lazy-loaded images without gaps or duplicated content. ### 2. Can I capture a full page screenshot without installing an extension?
Yes. Chrome DevTools has a built-in command to capture a full size screenshot without extensions. Open DevTools, open the Command Menu, type screenshot, and select Capture full size screenshot. It saves a high-quality PNG locally with no permissions required. The tradeoff is no PDF export, no annotation, and a few extra clicks versus a toolbar button for daily use.

### 3. Which extensions export full page captures to PDF with selectable text?
FireShot provides the best PDF export, preserving selectable text and hyperlinks and supporting both single-page and multi-page PDFs. GoFullPage and Nimbus also export to PDF, with GoFullPage offering searchable single-page PDF on the free plan. If you need PDFs for documentation or client reports, prioritize FireShot for its link preservation and header/footer controls.

### 4. Are full page screenshot extensions safe and private?
Safety depends on permissions and processing location. Prefer extensions that process captures locally and request only activeTab, scripting, and downloads permissions. Local-only tools like GoFullPage and FullPage Capture never upload your screenshots. Cloud-first tools upload to their servers for sharing, so review their data collection, retention, deletion, and encryption policies before capturing sensitive pages. Always check the Chrome Web Store privacy practices section.

### 5. Why does my full page screenshot have blurred sections or missing images?
Blurred or missing sections are usually caused by lazy-loaded images, animations, or insufficient scroll delay. Increase the scroll delay in the extension settings to give images time to load, disable smooth scroll animations, and ensure the page has fully loaded before capturing. Extensions that wait for network idle and handle sticky headers, like GoFullPage and Nimbus, reduce these artifacts significantly on long pages.

### 6. Do I need a paid plan for watermark-free full page screenshots?
No. The top recommendations including GoFullPage, FireShot, Nimbus, and FullPage Capture all offer watermark-free full-page capture on their free tiers for PNG and PDF. Paid plans unlock advanced editing, video recording, cloud storage, batch URLs, and team features. For most individuals and students, the free version is sufficient for daily full-page captures.

### 7. Which extension is best for annotating and blurring sensitive data after capture?
Nimbus and Awesome Screenshot offer the strongest built-in editors with arrows, text, shapes, and blur or redaction tools for hiding sensitive data. Nimbus is particularly strong for local blur and redaction before sharing. FireShot also has a capable local editor. If you need to capture dashboards with private information, choose a tool with local redaction so you never upload unredacted images.

### 8. How do screenshot extensions affect Chrome performance and page load time?
Extensions can affect browser performance by running background scripts and injecting content scripts on every page. Lightweight extensions under 300KB that activate only on click have negligible impact, while heavier video-enabled extensions use more memory. You can measure overhead with the Chrome DevTools Performance panel. To keep Chrome fast, keep only one screenshot extension enabled, disable it when not needed, and prefer local processing over always-on cloud sync.
