## What Is Hotspot Shield? (The Controversial Veteran)

Hotspot Shield is one of the oldest VPN services, founded in 2008 by AnchorFree (now Pango, a subsidiary of Aura). It's been downloaded over 650 million times, making it one of the most popular VPNs ever.

But popularity doesn't equal trustworthiness. Hotspot Shield has a complicated history that potential users need to understand.

### The Company Behind It

| Entity | Role |
|--------|------|
| **AnchorFree** | Original founder (2008) |
| **Pango** | Parent company (rebranded AnchorFree) |
| **Aura** | Parent of Pango (public company, NASDAQ: AURA) |
| **Location** | Redwood City, California, USA |

**The US jurisdiction is a red flag.** The United States is part of Five Eyes, Nine Eyes, and Fourteen Eyes surveillance alliances. US companies can be compelled by law to hand over user data. While Hotspot Shield claims no-logs, their privacy policy has enough wiggle room to concern privacy advocates.

### The 2016 Privacy Scandal

In 2016, the Center for Democracy & Technology (CDT) filed a complaint with the FTC alleging that Hotspot Shield:

- **Injected JavaScript into webpages** to serve ads
- **Redirected affiliate traffic** to earn commissions
- **Collected and sold user data** to third-party advertisers
- **Misrepresented its privacy protections**

**Hotspot Shield's response:** They claimed the allegations were about their free mobile app (not the VPN service) and that practices had changed. But the damage to their reputation was significant.

### The 2017 CSIRO Study

A study by Australia's Commonwealth Scientific and Industrial Research Organisation (CSIRO) found:

- Hotspot Shield's Android app contained **malware-like behavior**
- It requested **excessive permissions** (contacts, location, device ID)
- It **leaked DNS queries** outside the VPN tunnel
- It **failed to encrypt traffic** in some configurations

**Hotspot Shield's response:** They claimed the study tested an outdated version and that issues were fixed. But again, trust was eroded.

## FAQ

### Is Hotspot Shield's Chrome extension safe?
Marginally safe for casual browsing, but not for privacy-sensitive activities. The extension uses AES-256 encryption, but the company's privacy history and US jurisdiction raise concerns. For serious privacy, use NordVPN or ProtonVPN.

### Does Hotspot Shield keep logs?
Hotspot Shield claims no-logs, but there's no independent audit verifying this. Their privacy policy allows collection of device info, approximate location, and usage data. Without an audit, their claims are unverified.

### Is Hotspot Shield free tier worth using?
Only for very light use. The 500 MB/day limit is restrictive — roughly 30 minutes of standard YouTube. Speeds are throttled. No streaming support. For anything beyond casual browsing, upgrade to Premium or switch to ProtonVPN's unlimited free tier.

### Why is Hotspot Shield so fast?
The proprietary Catapult Hydra protocol is optimized for speed. It's 40-50% faster than OpenVPN in our tests. However, it's closed-source, so we can't verify its security claims or confirm it doesn't have backdoors.

### Did Hotspot Shield sell user data?
In 2016, the Center for Democracy & Technology filed an FTC complaint alleging Hotspot Shield injected ads, redirected affiliate traffic, and collected user data for advertising. Hotspot Shield denied the allegations but changed practices. No legal action resulted.

### Is Hotspot Shield better than NordVPN?
No. NordVPN is faster (485 Mbps vs 356 Mbps), cheaper ($3.39 vs $7.99/month), more trustworthy (audited no-logs, Panama jurisdiction), and has a larger server network. Hotspot Shield's only advantage is its free tier (which is very limited).

### Can Hotspot Shield unblock Netflix?
Premium users can sometimes unblock Netflix, but it's inconsistent. In our testing, it worked 6/10 attempts. NordVPN and ExpressVPN are far more reliable for streaming.

### Does Hotspot Shield work in China?
No. Hotspot Shield lacks obfuscation technology to hide VPN traffic from China's Great Firewall. For China, use specialized services like Astrill VPN or NordVPN's obfuscated servers.

### Is Hotspot Shield good for torrenting?
Premium users can torrent, but there are better options. NordVPN's dedicated P2P servers are faster and more trustworthy. Hotspot Shield's privacy concerns make it a poor choice for P2P activities.

### What's the best alternative to Hotspot Shield?
For speed + privacy: NordVPN. For free unlimited: ProtonVPN. For streaming: ExpressVPN. For value: Surfshark. All are cheaper, more trustworthy, and perform as well or better than Hotspot Shield.
