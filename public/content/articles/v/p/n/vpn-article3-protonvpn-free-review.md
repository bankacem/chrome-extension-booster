## The Limitations You Need to Know

ProtonVPN Free isn't perfect. Here are the honest limitations:

### 1. Only 5 Server Locations
Free users get: US, Netherlands, Japan, Romania, Poland. That's it. If you need to appear in the UK, Canada, Australia, or most other countries, you'll need Plus.

### 2. One Device at a Time
Only one active connection per account. If you're using it on your laptop, your phone can't connect simultaneously. Paid plans allow 10 connections.

### 3. No P2P/Torrenting
Torrenting is blocked on free servers. If you need to download Linux ISOs or other legal torrents, you'll need Plus or another VPN.

### 4. No Secure Core
Secure Core routes traffic through privacy-friendly countries before exiting. This protects against sophisticated attacks. Free users don't get this.

### 5. No Tor Over VPN
Accessing Tor through ProtonVPN adds an extra privacy layer. Free users can't use this feature.

### 6. Slower Speeds During Peak Hours
Free servers get congested. During evening hours (6-10 PM), expect 20-30% slower speeds than our baseline tests.

### 7. No Split Tunneling
Can't route some apps through VPN and others through regular connection. This is a Plus feature.

## FAQ

### Is ProtonVPN Chrome extension really free?
Yes, genuinely free with no data cap. You get unlimited bandwidth across 5 server countries. The free tier is funded by paid subscribers, not by selling your data.

### Does ProtonVPN free sell my data?
No. ProtonVPN's no-logs policy was verified in a Swiss court case. They have no data to sell. The company is funded by paid subscriptions, not data monetization.

### Why does ProtonVPN free require signup?
ProtonVPN needs an account to enforce the 1-device limit and offer premium upgrades. Unlike no-signup VPNs that harvest data anonymously, ProtonVPN's signup creates accountability. Your email is the only required information.

### Can I use ProtonVPN free for Netflix?
Sometimes. ProtonVPN free US servers occasionally work with Netflix, but streaming services actively block VPN IPs. For reliable Netflix access, you'll need Plus or a dedicated streaming VPN like NordVPN.

### Is ProtonVPN free good for torrenting?
No. P2P traffic is blocked on free servers. For torrenting, upgrade to Plus or use Windscribe's free tier (which allows P2P on 10GB/month).

### How does ProtonVPN free compare to paid VPNs?
ProtonVPN free offers better privacy than most paid VPNs (audited no-logs, Swiss jurisdiction, open source). However, it lacks features like multiple connections, P2P support, and extensive server networks. For pure privacy on a budget, it's unmatched.

### Will ProtonVPN free slow down my internet?
Yes, but minimally. Expect 20-35% speed reduction vs your base connection. During peak hours, free servers can be 40-50% slower due to congestion. For casual browsing and streaming, this is imperceptible.

### Can I use ProtonVPN free on multiple devices?
One device at a time per account. You can install the extension on multiple devices, but only one can be connected simultaneously. Upgrade to Plus for 10 connections.

### Is the ProtonVPN Chrome extension safe?
Yes. The extension uses the same AES-256 encryption and WireGuard protocol as the full app. It's open-source (auditable on GitHub) and developed by CERN scientists with a verified security track record.

### What's the difference between ProtonVPN extension and app?
The Chrome extension only protects browser traffic. The full app encrypts all device traffic (other browsers, apps, system processes). For complete protection, use both or just the full app.

### Does ProtonVPN free work in China?
Sometimes, but unreliably. Free servers lack obfuscation technology that hides VPN traffic. For China and other restrictive countries, Plus with Stealth protocol is recommended.

### Can I get ProtonVPN free without a credit card?
Yes. The free tier requires only an email address. No payment information needed. You can use the free tier indefinitely without ever entering a credit card.
