## What Makes ExpressVPN's Chrome Extension Different

Most VPN Chrome extensions are glorified proxies. They change your IP address but don't truly encrypt traffic. ExpressVPN's extension is different — it's a **full VPN remote control**.

### How It Works

1. Install the ExpressVPN desktop app (required)
2. Install the Chrome extension
3. The extension controls the desktop app — all traffic is encrypted at the OS level
4. Browser traffic gets the same AES-256 encryption as the full app

**This is both a strength and weakness.** The extension can't work alone, but when paired with the app, it offers true VPN protection — not just IP masking.

### Unique Extension Features

## The Downsides Nobody Talks About

### 1. Price: $99/Year Is Steep
ExpressVPN costs $8.32/month on annual plans — nearly 3x NordVPN's $3.39/month. For general VPN use, that's hard to justify.

### 2. Requires Full App Installation
Unlike NordVPN or Surfshark extensions that work standalone, ExpressVPN's extension is useless without the desktop app. This defeats the purpose of a "lightweight" browser extension.

### 3. Lightway Is Closed Source
ExpressVPN's proprietary protocol is fast but not independently auditable. NordLynx (WireGuard) is open-source and similarly fast.

### 4. Only 8 Simultaneous Connections
NordVPN allows 10. Surfshark allows unlimited. ExpressVPN's 8-device limit feels restrictive for families.

### 5. No Built-in Ad Blocker
Unlike NordVPN's Threat Protection or ProtonVPN's NetShield, ExpressVPN doesn't block ads or trackers at the DNS level.

## FAQ

### Is ExpressVPN's Chrome extension worth the price?
If you primarily need reliable streaming unblocking (Netflix, BBC iPlayer), yes — ExpressVPN is the most consistent. For general privacy, browsing, and occasional streaming, NordVPN at $3.39/month offers better value.

### Does ExpressVPN Chrome extension work without the desktop app?
No. The extension requires the ExpressVPN desktop application to be installed and running. It acts as a remote control for the app, not a standalone VPN. This is different from NordVPN and Surfshark extensions that work independently.

### Is ExpressVPN faster than NordVPN?
In our tests, NordVPN was 15% faster on average (485 Mbps vs 423 Mbps). However, ExpressVPN was more consistent across all tested servers. NordVPN had higher peaks but also lower valleys.

### Can ExpressVPN unblock Netflix in 2026?
Yes, reliably. ExpressVPN unblocks Netflix US, UK, Japan, Canada, and 10+ other regions. In 6 weeks of testing, it failed zero times. This is ExpressVPN's primary competitive advantage.

### Is ExpressVPN safe for torrenting?
Yes, but only through the full desktop app (not the extension alone). ExpressVPN allows P2P on all servers, has a kill switch, and maintains a verified no-logs policy. However, NordVPN's dedicated P2P servers are faster for torrenting.

### Does ExpressVPN keep logs?
No. ExpressVPN's no-logs policy has been independently audited by PwC and Cure53. They're based in the British Virgin Islands, outside surveillance alliances. In 2026, no verified logging incidents have occurred.

### What's the difference between ExpressVPN extension and app?
The extension controls the app and adds browser-specific features (HTTPS Everywhere, WebRTC blocking, location spoofing). The app provides system-wide protection. You need both for full functionality — the extension alone does nothing.

### Can I get ExpressVPN cheaper?
ExpressVPN occasionally offers 3-month free promotions on annual plans. The best price is typically $99.95/year ($8.32/month). They don't offer multi-year discounts like NordVPN. Student discounts are available through Student Beans.

### Does ExpressVPN work in China?
Yes, but inconsistently. ExpressVPN works in China but requires manual configuration and specific servers. For reliable China access, specialized services like Astrill VPN perform better. ExpressVPN is adequate for occasional China travel.

### Is ExpressVPN good for gaming?
ExpressVPN adds 15-25ms latency — acceptable for casual gaming but not ideal for competitive play. The Lightway protocol minimizes lag, but any VPN increases ping. For gaming, a wired connection without VPN is always best.

### How does ExpressVPN compare to free VPNs?
ExpressVPN is dramatically better than any free VPN in every category: speed, security, streaming, reliability, and support. The question isn't whether ExpressVPN is better — it's whether the improvement is worth $99/year vs free or cheaper alternatives.
