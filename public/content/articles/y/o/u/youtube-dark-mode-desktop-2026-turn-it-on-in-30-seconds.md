## Step-by-Step Guide to Enable Dark Mode on YouTube Desktop

![Youtube Dark Mode Desktop 2026 Turn It On In 30 Seconds Overview](/content/images/youtube-dark-mode-desktop-2026-turn-it-on-in-30-seconds/youtube-dark-mode-desktop-2026-turn-it-on-in-30-seconds-overview.webp "Youtube Dark Mode Desktop 2026 Turn It On In 30 Seconds Overview")


To enable Dark Mode on YouTube desktop, follow these simple steps:

1. Open YouTube on your desktop and sign in to your account.
2. Click on your profile picture in the top right corner of the screen.
3. Click on the "Appearance" option from the dropdown menu.
4. Toggle the switch next to "Dark theme" to enable Dark Mode.
5. Once you've enabled Dark Mode, you'll notice that the YouTube interface has changed to a darker color scheme.

That's it! You've successfully enabled Dark Mode on YouTube desktop. If you want to switch back to the default theme, simply follow the same steps and toggle the switch off.
