## What are YouTube Productivity Extensions?
YouTube productivity extensions are browser add-ons designed to help you stay focused, avoid distractions, and make the most of your time on the platform. These extensions can block annoying ads, provide keyboard shortcuts, and even help you schedule your video watching. By using the best YouTube productivity extensions, you can boost your productivity, reduce eye strain, and achieve your goals faster.
