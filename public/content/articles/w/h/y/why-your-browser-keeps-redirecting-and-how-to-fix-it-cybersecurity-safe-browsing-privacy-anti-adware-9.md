## Causes of Browser Redirection
There are several reasons **why your browser keeps redirecting**. Some of the most common causes include:

- Malware infections: Malware can infect your device and cause browser redirection.

 - Adware: Adware can also cause browser redirection, often to display unwanted ads or promotions.

 - [Browser extensions](/blog/the-elite-stack-essential-chrome-extensions-for-work-pro-environments): Malicious browser extensions can cause browser redirection.

 - Router hijacking: In some cases, your router can be hijacked, causing browser redirection.

To prevent browser redirection, it is essential to use a reputable anti-virus software and keep your browser and operating system up to date. You can also use a Chrome extension like [Redirect Shield](/extension/redirect-shield) to stop automatic redirects and protect yourself from malicious chains.
