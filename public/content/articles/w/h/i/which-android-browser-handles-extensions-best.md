## Features to Consider
When choosing the **best android browser for extensions**, there are several features to consider, including:

- Extension support: Look for browsers that support a wide range of extensions, including those that are similar to Chrome extensions.

 - Speed and [performance](/blog/unlocking-peak-performance-browser-optimization-extensions): A fast and responsive browser is essential for a smooth browsing experience.

By considering these features, you can find the **best android browser for extensions** that meets your needs and enhances your mobile browsing experience.
