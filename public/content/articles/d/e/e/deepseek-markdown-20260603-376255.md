## 🏆 #1: LLMnesia — The Cross-Platform Champion

**Free | 472KiB | Updated May 18, 2026 | Chrome Web Store** [citation:1]

This is the newest — and most powerful — search extension on the list. LLMnesia does what no other tool does: **searches all your AI conversations across every platform at once**.

### What Makes It Special

**One search bar. Every AI platform. Instant results.**

Type a keyword, and LLMnesia searches your conversation history across:
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini
- Perplexity
- DeepSeek

**You don't need to remember which platform you used.** You don't need to remember what you named the conversation. Just type what you remember, and LLMnesia finds it [citation:1].

### How It Works

LLMnesia is a Chrome extension that **indexes your AI conversations locally in your browser**. Everything stays on your machine. No account required. No data leaves your device. No cloud. No tracking [citation:1].

Click a search result and you're taken straight to the original conversation. No copying, no exporting, no syncing to the cloud.

### Privacy

**This is the best part:** "Everything stays on your machine. No account required. No data leaves your device. No cloud. No tracking. Your conversation history is searchable only by you." [citation:1]

### The Catch

- New extension (fewer user reviews than established tools)
- Currently supports 5 platforms — more coming

### Verdict

**The best choice for anyone using multiple AI platforms.** If you use ChatGPT sometimes and Claude other times, LLMnesia is a game-changer.

## 🔎 #2: 1Proompt — Best for ChatGPT-Only Users

**Free | 869KiB | Updated January 13, 2026 | Chrome Web Store** [citation:6]

1Proompt focuses on one thing and does it well: **searching your ChatGPT conversation history**.

### What Makes It Special

**Advanced fuzzy matching algorithm** [citation:6]

Here's what that means: You remember a conversation about "that blue car company with the weird CEO." ChatGPT's built-in search would find nothing. 1Proompt's fuzzy matching finds it anyway, even if your keywords aren't exact.

### Key Features

- **Save 💾** — All your ChatGPT conversation records stored locally, accessible even without internet connection
- **Search 🔍** — Full-text searches using advanced fuzzy matching algorithm
- **Organize 📁** — Folders and tags (coming soon, currently rolling out) [citation:6]

### Privacy

All features executed locally, as they should be [citation:6].

### The Catch

- ChatGPT only (no Claude, Gemini, Perplexity)
- Folder organization is "coming soon" — not yet available

### Verdict

**Best for heavy ChatGPT users who don't use other AI platforms.** The fuzzy matching is a killer feature for finding conversations when you only remember vague details.

## 🎨 #3: StylerGPT — The All-in-One (But Search Isn't Primary)

**Freemium | 5.26MiB | Updated May 17, 2026 | Chrome Web Store** [citation:3]

StylerGPT is primarily a **theme and organization tool** — 1000+ themes, chat folders, prompt manager, export tools. It includes search functionality, but that's not its main focus.

### What It Offers for Search

- **Prompt History** — Ctrl/Cmd + Up/Down to cycle through previous prompts
- **Message Navigator** — Jump to first, previous, next, or last message
- **Message timestamps** — See when each message was sent [citation:3]

### The Catch

- Full conversation search is not the primary feature
- 5.26MiB (much larger than LLMnesia's 472KiB)
- Freemium model — some features require payment

### Verdict

**Best if you want everything in one tool** (themes + folders + prompts + basic search). For pure search, LLMnesia or 1Proompt are better.
