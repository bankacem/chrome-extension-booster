## 🩸 The "Invisible Threat" (How to Spot a Fake)

**This is the section that will make you an expert.**
In February 2026, security firm LayerX exposed the "AiFrame" operation. Hackers created clones of ChatGPT/Gemini extensions.

**How they trick you:**
They ask for permissions to "read and change all your data on mail.google.com."
**What they actually do:** They inject an invisible frame (iframe) that silently copies your draft emails, your boss's emails, and even your login cookies to their server in real-time.

✅ **The Golden Rule of 2026 Safety:**
- **Safe:** Extensions that only request access to `activeTab` or specific APIs.
- **Dangerous:** Extensions that request `Host permissions` for `*.google.com` *without* a verified publisher badge.
