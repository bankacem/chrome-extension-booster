## Privacy & Security Guide

**Before installing any AI translator extension, check these 3 things:**

| Red Flag | Safe Alternative |
|----------|------------------|
| Sends every page you visit to their server | Processes translation only when you click |
| Requires "read all your data on all websites" | Requests "active tab only" permission |
| No privacy policy or vague wording | Clear policy on data deletion (7-30 days) |

**Recommended permission level:**  
- ✅ Access to active tab only (not all websites)  
- ✅ Translation triggered manually (not automatic)  
- ✅ Option to disable data collection  
- ❌ Never "all websites" permission  
- ❌ Never request access to browsing history  

**Pro privacy move:** Use the extension in **incognito mode** (enable in `chrome://extensions` → Details → Allow in incognito) to ensure no translation history is saved.

**Warning:** Free AI translators often sell anonymized translation data to train their models. If you translate sensitive documents (legal, medical, financial), upgrade to a paid tier with zero-data retention policies.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there a completely free AI translator with no character limits?
No. Server costs exist. The most generous free tier in 2026 is **LinguAI (100,000 characters/month)** — roughly 40-50 full webpages. For unlimited, paid plans start at $9–20/month.

### Can AI translator handle slang and idioms?
Yes — AI translators (LinguAI, Translate Pro) use large language models that understand context. "It is raining cats and dogs" translates to "It is raining heavily" in most languages. Old translators (Google Translate) fail at this.

### Which extension is best for Arabic translation?
**LinguAI** — supports Modern Standard Arabic plus dialect detection (Egyptian, Levantine, Gulf). Translate Pro AI is also good but lacks dialect support.

### Can I translate images or screenshots?
No — Chrome extensions translate text only. For images, right-click → "Search image with Google Lens" → copy text from image → paste into translator.

### Does AI translator work on Netflix subtitles?
Yes — but only if subtitles are rendered as text (not burned into video). Enable subtitles in your language, then use extension to translate to your target language in real time.

### Will translation quality decrease for rare languages?
Yes — rare languages (Swahili, Icelandic, Basque) have less training data. Quality is still better than Google Translate but worse than common languages (Spanish, Chinese, Arabic). For rare languages, DeepL is often better despite lower limits.

### Can I translate both ways?
Yes — click the swap icon (↔️) in the extension to reverse language direction. Example: English → Arabic → Arabic → English.

### How accurate is AI translation in 2026?
For common language pairs (English-Spanish, English-Chinese, English-Arabic): **95% accuracy** for basic text, **85% for technical** content. Always proofread important translations.

### Which extension has the fastest translation speed?
**LinguAI** — uses a compressed model, translates full pages in 2-3 seconds. Translate Pro is slower (5-7 seconds) but more accurate for technical content.

### Can I use AI translator on Chrome for Android?
No — Chrome extensions only work on desktop Chrome. Mobile users can install Microsoft Edge (Android) which has built-in AI translation.

### How do I remove a translator extension?
Right-click extension icon → Remove from Chrome. Or go to `chrome://extensions` → Remove.

### What happens when I hit the monthly character limit?
The extension stops translating and shows: "Free limit reached. Upgrade or wait until next month." Some extensions offer 10,000 bonus characters for sharing on social media.

### Can I translate WhatsApp Web messages?
Yes — open web.whatsapp.com, select any message or conversation, right-click → Translate with extension.

### Which extension is best for business documents?
**Translate Pro AI** — includes business terminology database (legal, financial, medical). More accurate for contracts and technical specs.

### Does AI translator work offline?
Some extensions (LinguAI) offer limited offline mode with 10 pre-downloaded languages. Full quality requires internet.
