## 🪶 #1: Plume AI — The Privacy-First Open Source Option

**Free (with API key) | Open source | Updated April 2026 | Chrome & Firefox** [citation:4]

Plume AI was built because the developer was "tired of the 'context switch' — opening a new tab, pasting text, asking for a grammar check, and pasting it back" [citation:4].

### What Makes It Special

**You own the brain:** No monthly subscriptions. Connect your own API key (Gemini, Claude, OpenAI, Mistral) or run it **100% locally with Ollama** [citation:4].

**Privacy isn't an afterthought:** Your text is anonymized (emails/phones stripped) before it ever hits an API. The original text is restored after the check [citation:4].

**Native explanations:** It explains grammar mistakes in *your* native language, even if you're writing in another one [citation:4].

### Key Features

#### Grammar Check
Click the **✦** trigger button that appears when you focus any text field — or press **`Ctrl+Shift+G`** to open the panel instantly. The extension analyses your text and highlights every grammar, spelling, and word-choice error inline. Each highlight shows the suggested fix and a short explanation. Hit **Fix all** to apply every correction at once [citation:4].

**Text language support:** English (US/UK), French, German, Spanish, Dutch — or **auto-detect** [citation:4].

#### Interface Language (i18n)
The extension UI and error explanations adapt to your chosen interface language — independently of the language you are writing in. Set your native language once; the AI will explain grammar mistakes in that language even if the text you are correcting is in a different one [citation:4].

**Supported interface languages:** English, English (UK), French, German, Spanish, Dutch, Portuguese (PT), Portuguese (BR), Chinese Simplified, **Arabic**, **Persian**, Japanese [citation:4].

#### AI Rewrite
Hit **✦ Polish** to let the AI rewrite your full text (or just the selected portion) for clarity and natural flow. Review the suggestion, then accept or dismiss [citation:4].

#### Tone Presets
Four one-click tone pills [citation:4]:

| Pill | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| **Shorter** | Cuts filler, keeps the meaning |
| **Formal** | Professional tone |
| **Direct** | Removes hedging and filler phrases |
| **Technical** | Precise, domain-appropriate language |

#### Translation
Translate the field's content into any supported language in one click — right from the panel, without leaving the page [citation:4].

#### Accessibility
Built to WCAG 2.1 AA [citation:4]:
- Full keyboard navigation
- Screen reader support (`aria-live` announcements)
- Focus-visible indicators
- 4.5:1 minimum contrast ratio
- Respects OS-level reduced motion preference

### Providers & Setup

Connect any provider in the popup. Each requires its own API key or local setup [citation:4]:

| Provider | Where to get a key | Notes |
|----------|-------------------|-------|
| **Gemini** | aistudio.google.com | Free tier available |
| **Claude** | console.anthropic.com | Requires billing |
| **ChatGPT (OpenAI)** | platform.openai.com | |
| **Mistral** | console.mistral.ai | |
| **Ollama** | — | Run locally |

Use the **Test connection** button to verify your key before saving [citation:4].

### The Catch

- Requires API key setup (not plug-and-play)
- Local Ollama setup requires technical knowledge

### Verdict

**The best choice for privacy-conscious users and multilingual writers.** The ability to explain grammar mistakes in Arabic while you write in English is unique.

## 📚 #2: Trinka AI — For Academic & Technical Writing

**Completely free | 435KiB | Updated February 25, 2026 | Chrome Web Store** [citation:9]

Trinka AI is designed specifically for **academic and technical writing** — not general content.

### What Makes It Special

**Next-gen language & grammar checker for academic writing** [citation:9]

"Academic writing needs to be clear, concise, formal, objective, and readable. Trinka... corrects complex grammar errors and goes the extra mile by enhancing your writing for vocabulary, tone, and conciseness" [citation:9].

### Key Features

- **Corrects complex grammar errors** unique to academic writing that other tools don't find
- **Enhances vocabulary, tone, and conciseness**
- **Real-time editing** as you type
- **Add to dictionary** — Add your own words to "My Dictionary"
- **Corrects biased or vague language** [citation:9]

### Where It Works

- Academic writing platforms (Authorea, typeset.io)
- Most websites [citation:9]

### Privacy

"Your data is encrypted with the latest security standards and processed in a highly secure environment. All data transmission between your browser and our servers is secure. Your data is not shared with any third party." [citation:9]

### The Catch

- English only (no multilingual support)
- Designed for academic/technical writing (overkill for casual emails)
- Cloud-based (text sent to their servers)

### Verdict

**Best for students, researchers, and academics.** If you're writing a paper, thesis, or technical document, Trinka catches errors Grammarly misses.
