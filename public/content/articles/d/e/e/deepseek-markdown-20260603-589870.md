**No installation required | Chrome 138+ | Available in US (English)[citation: 10]**

Google has quietly embedded Gemini directly into Chrome. No extension needed.

### How to Access It

1. Make sure you have Chrome 138 or later
2. Click the **Gemini icon** in the top-right toolbar
3. Or press **Alt + G** (Windows) to open the sidebar[citation: 10]

### What It Can Do

- **Read your current tab** — Ask "summarize this article"
- **Work across multiple tabs** — Ask "what's the main point of all these tabs?"
- **Find specific info** — "Find the price of this laptop on this page"[citation: 10]

### The Catch

- Currently only available in the US with browser language set to English
- Requires Google One AI subscription for advanced features
- Cloud-based (your data goes to Google)

**Verdict: ** Try this first if you meet the requirements. It's free, requires no installation, and works surprisingly well.
