<p>Tab suspension is a process that temporarily puts inactive tabs to sleep, freeing up system resources and reducing memory usage. This feature is particularly useful for users who tend to have multiple tabs open simultaneously, such as researchers, developers, or anyone who needs to access multiple web pages at once. By suspending inactive tabs, you can prevent them from consuming valuable system resources, resulting in a faster and more responsive browsing experience.</p>
