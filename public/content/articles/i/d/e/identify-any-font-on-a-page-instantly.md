## What is a Font Finder Extension?

A **font finder extension Chrome** is a browser extension designed to help users identify the fonts used on a website. These extensions typically work by analyzing the website's CSS and HTML code to detect the fonts used for different elements, such as headings, paragraphs, and buttons. With a font finder extension, you can easily discover the fonts used on your favorite websites, inspiration boards, or design references, and even download them for use in your own projects.
