<p>Cookies can be categorized into different types based on their purpose and functionality. First-party cookies are set by the website you are visiting and are generally used for authentication, session management, and personalization. Third-party cookies, on the other hand, are set by external domains and are often used for tracking and advertising purposes. Managing these cookies is crucial to maintaining your online privacy and security. An effective <strong>extension chrome cookie manager</strong> can help you block unwanted cookies, manage cookie permissions, and automatically delete cookies when you close your browser.</p>
