## What Is a Free AI Content Summarizer Extension?

![What a free AI content summarizer extension does — long document condensed to key points](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1620712943543-bcc4688e7485?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

A free AI content summarizer extension is a browser add-on that condenses any web page — news article, research paper, documentation, or blog post — into a short digest without you leaving the tab. Instead of copying text into a chatbot, you click one button and get the key points in seconds.

The category split into three camps in 2026:

- **Local-AI tools** that run on Chrome's built-in Gemini Nano model, keeping everything on your device
- **API-based tools** where you plug in your own model keys and choose quality versus cost
- **Platform-native features** like Google's embedded Gemini sidebar that need no extension at all

Each camp has real trade-offs around privacy, quality, and setup effort. Here's how the leading options compare in practice.

## Frequently Asked Questions

![Free AI content summarizer extension FAQ — questions about AI tools](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1535378917042-10a22c95931a?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

### Are free AI summarizer extensions really free?

The three options in this guide are free in different ways. Free AI Summarizer runs on Chrome's local model at no cost. Chrome's built-in Gemini is free with optional paid upgrades. The AI Summary Extension is open-source but charges nothing only when you use free models or your own API keys — paid models bill per token.

### Does a summarizer extension send my data to the cloud?

It depends on the tool. Free AI Summarizer uses Chrome's on-device Gemini Nano, so page content never leaves your machine. Chrome's built-in Gemini is cloud-based, and API-based tools send text to whichever model provider you configure. Check the privacy panel in each tool's settings before summarizing sensitive documents.

### What Chrome version do I need for local AI summaries?

Chrome 138 or later is required for the built-in Gemini Nano features that power local summarization. Check chrome://settings/help to see your current version and update if needed.

### Can a free AI content summarizer extension handle PDFs?

Most extensions summarize HTML pages reliably; PDF support varies by tool because the extension needs to extract text from the viewer. For PDFs, either open them in Chrome's native viewer and test your extension, or copy the relevant section into a chat interface that accepts document uploads.

### Will summarizing an article reduce its accuracy?

Summaries compress, so nuance can be lost — that's inherent to the technology, not the specific extension. Use summaries to decide what deserves a full read, not as a substitute for reading sources you'll cite or act on.

### Do these extensions work on Firefox or Edge?

The extensions covered here are built for Chrome's extension APIs. Chromium-based browsers like Edge often run them too, but the local Gemini Nano feature is Chrome-specific. Firefox users need tools built on the WebExtensions API that explicitly support that browser.
