## What are Popups?

Popups are small windows that appear on your screen, usually with advertisements, offers, or alerts. While some popups can be useful, such as those providing important information or warnings, many are intrusive and unwanted. They can be triggered by visiting certain websites, clicking on links, or even by malware. The primary goal of a **Chrome extension to stop popups** is to block these unwanted windows, ensuring your browsing experience remains uninterrupted.
