## How to Disable Chrome Extensions

![How To Disable Chrome Extensions Simplifying Your Browser Overview](/content/images/how-to-disable-chrome-extensions-simplifying-your-browser/how-to-disable-chrome-extensions-simplifying-your-browser-overview.webp "How To Disable Chrome Extensions Simplifying Your Browser Overview")


So, **how to disable Chrome extensions**? Fortunately, it's a relatively straightforward process. Here's a step-by-step guide to get you started:

1. Open Google Chrome and click on the three vertical dots in the upper right corner of the browser window.
2. From the drop-down menu, select "More tools" and then "Extensions".
3. This will take you to the Chrome extensions page, where you can view and manage all of your installed extensions.
4. To disable an extension, simply toggle the switch next to it to the "off" position.
5. To remove an extension, click on the "Remove" button next to it.
