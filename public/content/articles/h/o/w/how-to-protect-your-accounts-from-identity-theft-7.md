## What is Identity Theft?

Identity theft occurs when an individual or group of individuals illegally obtain and use someone else's personal data, such as their name, address, social security number, or credit card information, for their own gain. This can lead to a range of problems, including financial loss, damage to credit scores, and even legal issues. Understanding **how to protect your accounts from identity theft** is essential to preventing these types of situations.

### Types of Identity Theft

There are several types of identity theft, including:

- Financial identity theft: This involves the use of someone's financial information, such as credit card numbers or bank account details, to make unauthorized transactions.
- Medical identity theft: This occurs when someone uses another person's medical information to receive treatment or benefits.
- Driver's license identity theft: This involves the use of someone's driver's license information to commit crimes or obtain fake identification.
