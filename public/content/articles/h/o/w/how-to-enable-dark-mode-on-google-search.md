## What is Dark Mode?

Dark Mode, also known as night mode, is a display setting that replaces the traditional bright white background with a darker color scheme. This feature is designed to reduce eye strain and improve visibility in low-light [environments](/blog/the-elite-stack-essential-chrome-extensions-for-work-pro-environments "The Elite Stack: Essential Chrome Extensions for Work Pro Environments"). Many websites and apps, including Google Search, offer Dark Mode as an option to enhance user experience.

## How to Enable Dark Mode on Google Search

![How To Enable Dark Mode On Google Search Overview](/content/images/how-to-enable-dark-mode-on-google-search/how-to-enable-dark-mode-on-google-search-overview.webp "How To Enable Dark Mode On Google Search Overview")


To **enable Dark Mode on Google Search**, follow these simple steps:

1. Open Google Search in your web browser.
2. Click on the settings icon (represented by three vertical dots) in the top right corner of the page.
3. From the drop-down menu, select "Search settings."
4. Scroll down to the "Appearance" section.
5. Click on the "Dark theme" option to enable Dark Mode.
