## What are Chrome Extensions?

Chrome extensions are small software programs that can be installed in the Google Chrome browser to add new features, functionality, and improvements to the browsing experience. They can range from simple tools like [Redirect Shield](/extension/redirect-shield), which helps protect against malicious redirects, to complex applications like [Formula Builder Pro](/extension/formula-builder-pro), which allows you to build and calculate complex formulas directly in your browser.

Here are some frequently asked questions about Chrome extension download and usage:
