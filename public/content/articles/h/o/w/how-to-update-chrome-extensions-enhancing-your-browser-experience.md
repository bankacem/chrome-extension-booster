## How to Update Chrome Extensions

Updating Chrome extensions is a straightforward process. You can update extensions [automatically](/blog/stop-video-popups-from-playing-automatically-3 "Stop Video Popups from Playing Automatically: A Comprehensive Guide") or manually, depending on your preferences. Here's a step-by-step guide on **how to update Chrome extensions**:

### Automatic Updates

Chrome extensions can be set to update automatically. To enable automatic updates, follow these steps:

1. Open Google Chrome and click on the three vertical dots in the top right corner.
2. Click on "More tools" and then "Extensions".
3. Toggle the switch next to "Developer mode" to enable it.
4. Click on "Update" to update all extensions.

### Manual Updates

If you prefer to update your extensions manually, you can do so by following these steps:

1. Open Google Chrome and click on the three vertical dots in the top right corner.
2. Click on "More tools" and then "Extensions".
3. Click on the "Update" button next to the extension you want to update.
4. Alternatively, you can click on the "Details" button and then click on "Update" to update the extension.
