## What are Screenshots?

A screenshot, also known as a screen capture or screen grab, is an image that shows the contents of a computer screen at a particular moment. Screenshots can be used to demonstrate a point, illustrate a concept, or provide visual evidence of a bug or issue. In the context of **how to capture and share screenshots instantly**, screenshots are an essential tool for bloggers, web designers, bug reporters, and students who need to communicate complex ideas or report issues quickly and efficiently.
