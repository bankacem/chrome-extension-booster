<h2 id="pinterest">How to Enable Dark Mode on Pinterest</h2>
<p>To enable Dark Mode on Pinterest, follow these simple steps:</p>
<ol>
  <li>Log in to your Pinterest account and click on your profile picture in the top right corner.</li>
  <li>Click on the "Settings" icon (represented by a gear) and select "Account settings" from the dropdown menu.</li>
  <li>Scroll down to the "Appearance" section and toggle the "Dark mode" switch to the right.</li>
  <li>Click "Save" to apply the changes.</li>
</ol>

<h2 id="instagram">How to Enable Dark Mode on Instagram</h2>
<p>Enabling Dark Mode on Instagram is a bit more involved, but still easy to do:</p>
<ol>
  <li>Open the Instagram app on your mobile device and tap on your profile picture in the bottom right corner.</li>
  <li>Tap on the three horizontal lines (represented by a hamburger menu) and select "Settings" from the menu.</li>
  <li>Scroll down to the "Account" section and tap on "Theme".</li>
  <li>Select "Dark" from the available options.</li>
</ol>

<h2 id="netflix">How to Enable Dark Mode on Netflix</h2>
<p>To enable Dark Mode on Netflix, follow these steps:</p>
<ol>
  <li>Log in to your Netflix account and click on your profile picture in the top right corner.</li>
  <li>Click on the "Account" dropdown menu and select "Profile & Parental Controls".</li>
  <li>Scroll down to the "Profile" section and click on the "Change" button next to "Appearance".</li>
  <li>Select "Dark" from the available options and click "Save" to apply the changes.</li>
</ol>

<h2 id="github">How to Enable Dark Mode on GitHub</h2>
<p>Enabling Dark Mode on GitHub is easy:</p>
<ol>
  <li>Log in to your GitHub account and click on your profile picture in the top right corner.</li>
  <li>Click on the "Settings" icon (represented by a gear) and select "Appearance" from the dropdown menu.</li>
  <li>Toggle the "Theme" switch to "Dark" and click "Update preferences" to apply the changes.</li>
</ol>

<h2 id="stackoverflow">How to Enable Dark Mode on StackOverflow</h2>
<p>To enable Dark Mode on StackOverflow, follow these steps:</p>
<ol>
  <li>Log in to your StackOverflow account and click on your profile picture in the top right corner.</li>
  <li>Click on the "Settings" icon (represented by a gear) and select "Preferences" from the dropdown menu.</li>
  <li>Scroll down to the "Display" section and toggle the "Dark mode" switch to the right.</li>
  <li>Click "Save and reload" to apply the changes.</li>
</ol>
