## How to Enable Dark Mode on Pinterest

To enable Dark Mode on Pinterest, follow these simple steps:

1. Log in to your Pinterest account and click on your profile picture in the top right corner.
2. Click on the "Settings" icon (represented by a gear) and select "Account settings" from the dropdown menu.
3. Scroll down to the "Appearance" section and toggle the "Dark mode" switch to the right.
4. Click "Save" to apply the changes.

## How to Enable Dark Mode on Instagram

Enabling Dark Mode on Instagram is a bit more involved, but still easy to do:

1. Open the Instagram app on your mobile device and tap on your profile picture in the bottom right corner.
2. Tap on the three horizontal lines (represented by a hamburger menu) and select "Settings" from the menu.
3. Scroll down to the "Account" section and tap on "Theme".
4. Select "Dark" from the available options.

To enable Dark Mode on Netflix, follow these steps:

1. Log in to your Netflix account and click on your profile picture in the top right corner.
2. Click on the "Account" dropdown menu and select "Profile & Parental Controls".
3. Scroll down to the "Profile" section and click on the "Change" button next to "Appearance".
4. Select "Dark" from the available options and click "Save" to apply the changes.

## How to Enable Dark Mode on GitHub

Enabling Dark Mode on GitHub is easy:

1. Log in to your GitHub account and click on your profile picture in the top right corner.
2. Click on the "Settings" icon (represented by a gear) and select "Appearance" from the dropdown menu.
3. Toggle the "Theme" switch to "Dark" and click "Update preferences" to apply the changes.

## How to Enable Dark Mode on StackOverflow

To enable Dark Mode on StackOverflow, follow these steps:

1. Log in to your StackOverflow account and click on your profile picture in the top right corner.
2. Click on the "Settings" icon (represented by a gear) and select "Preferences" from the dropdown menu.
3. Scroll down to the "Display" section and toggle the "Dark mode" switch to the right.
4. Click "Save and reload" to apply the changes.
