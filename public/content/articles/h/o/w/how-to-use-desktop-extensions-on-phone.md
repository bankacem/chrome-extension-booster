<h2 id="how-to-use-desktop-extensions-on-phone">How to Use Desktop Extensions on Phone</h2>

<p>To <strong>use desktop extensions on phone</strong>, you'll need to use a mobile browser that supports extensions, such as Chrome or Firefox. Here's a step-by-step guide:</p>

<ol>
    <li>Open your mobile browser and navigate to the extensions store.</li>
    <li>Search for the extension you want to install and click the "Add to Browser" button.</li>
    <li>Wait for the extension to download and install.</li>
    <li>Once installed, you can access the extension by clicking the three dots in the top right corner of your browser and selecting "Extensions."</li>
</ol>
