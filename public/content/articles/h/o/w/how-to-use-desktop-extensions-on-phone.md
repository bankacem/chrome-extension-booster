## How to Use Desktop Extensions on Phone

To **use desktop extensions on phone**, you'll need to use a mobile browser that supports extensions, such as Chrome or Firefox. Here's a step-by-step guide:

1. Open your mobile browser and navigate to the extensions store.
2. Search for the extension you want to install and click the "Add to Browser" button.
3. Wait for the extension to download and install.
4. Once installed, you can access the extension by clicking the three dots in the top right corner of your browser and selecting "Extensions."
