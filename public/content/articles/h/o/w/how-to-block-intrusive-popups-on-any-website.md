## What are Intrusive Popups?

Intrusive popups are unwanted windows or dialogs that appear on a website without the user's consent. They can be used to display advertisements, promotions, or other types of content that can be distracting or annoying. Intrusive popups can take many forms, including:

- Modal windows that overlay the main content of the website
- Pop-up windows that appear on top of the main browser window
- Interstitial ads that appear between page loads
- Slide-in ads that appear from the side of the screen

### How to Configure the Popup Blocker in Google Chrome

To configure the popup blocker in Google Chrome, follow these steps:

1. Open Google Chrome and click the three vertical dots in the top right corner of the browser window
2. Click "Settings" to open the browser's settings page
3. Scroll down to the "Privacy and security" section and click "Site settings"
4. Scroll down to the "Content" section and click "Pop-ups and redirects"
5. Configure the popup blocker settings to block popups on specific websites
