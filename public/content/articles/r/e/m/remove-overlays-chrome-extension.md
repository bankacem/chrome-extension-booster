## What are Overlays?

![Remove Overlays Chrome Extension Overview](/content/images/remove-overlays-chrome-extension/remove-overlays-chrome-extension-overview.webp "Remove Overlays Chrome Extension Overview")


Overlays are layers of content that appear on top of a web page, often used for advertising, [notifications](/blog/block-newsletter-popups-and-allow-notifications-prompts-5 "Block Newsletter Popups and "), or other interactive elements. While they can be useful in some cases, they can also be intrusive and distracting, covering important content and disrupting the user experience.

### Types of Overlays

There are several types of overlays that you may encounter while browsing the web, including:

- Popups: Small windows that appear on top of a web page, often used for advertising or notifications.
- Modals: Large windows that cover the entire screen, often used for login forms or other interactive elements.
- Banners: Horizontal or vertical strips of content that appear at the top or bottom of a web page, often used for advertising or promotions.
