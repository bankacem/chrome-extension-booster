## What are Chrome Extensions?

Chrome extensions are programs that can be installed into Chrome to change the browser's functionality. This can include anything from blocking annoying ads with [Light Popup Blocker](/extension/light-popup-blocker) to managing passwords securely with [SecuraKey Pro](/extension/securakey-pro). The key to getting the most out of **store extension chrome** is understanding your needs and finding the extensions that best meet them.

### Types of Chrome Extensions

There are several types of Chrome extensions available, catering to different needs and preferences. Some are designed for productivity, such as [ProTab Suspender](/extension/protab-suspender), which helps manage memory usage by suspending inactive tabs.
