## What is Ghostery?

Ghostery is a free, open-source browser extension that helps users block trackers, ads, and other online annoyances. By installing Ghostery, you can take control of your online data and protect your privacy. With its advanced tracking detection and blocking capabilities, Ghostery is an essential tool for anyone concerned about their online security.

## Key Features of Ghostery for Chrome Android

**Ghostery for Chrome Android** offers a range of features that make it an excellent choice for Android users. Some of the key features include:

- Tracker blocking: Ghostery blocks trackers from companies like Google, Facebook, and Twitter, ensuring that your online activities remain private.

 - Ad blocking: Ghostery also blocks ads, reducing clutter and improving your browsing experience.

 - Anti-tracking: Ghostery's anti-tracking feature prevents companies from collecting your data and building profiles about you.

 - Customizable: Ghostery allows you to customize your blocking settings, so you can choose which trackers to block and which to allow.
