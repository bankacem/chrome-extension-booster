## What is Avast Plugin Chrome?

The Avast plugin Chrome is a browser extension developed by Avast, a well-known cybersecurity company. This extension is designed to provide an additional layer of security and protection to your Chrome browser, safeguarding you against various online threats. By leveraging Avast's extensive database of malware and phishing sites, the Avast plugin Chrome helps in identifying and blocking harmful websites and downloads, thus reducing the risk of your device becoming infected.

### Key Components of the Avast Plugin Chrome

- **Website Scanning:** The Avast plugin Chrome scans websites for malware, phishing attempts, and other online threats, providing you with a safe browsing experience.

 - **Real-Time Protection:** It offers real-time protection against viruses, Trojans, and other types of malware, ensuring your device remains secure.
