## What are Chrome Extensions?
Chrome extensions are small software programs that can be installed on the Google Chrome browser to enhance its functionality. They can be used to perform a variety of tasks, such as blocking ads, managing passwords, and even helping with productivity. One of the most popular Chrome extensions for productivity is the [ProTab Suspender](/extension/protab-suspender). This extension helps to suspend inactive tabs, freeing up memory and reducing battery consumption.
