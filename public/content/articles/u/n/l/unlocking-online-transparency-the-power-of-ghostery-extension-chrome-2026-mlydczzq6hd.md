<p>Ghostery is a privacy-focused browser extension available for Chrome, among other browsers, that helps users detect and block tracking technologies used by websites. Ghostery achieves this through its comprehensive database of trackers, which is constantly updated to stay ahead of emerging tracking technologies.</p>

<h3>How Ghostery Works</h3>

<p>Upon installation, the Ghostery extension Chrome 2026 begins to scan the websites you visit for tracking scripts. These scripts can be from a variety of sources, including advertisers, analytics services, and social media platforms. Once identified, Ghostery blocks these scripts, preventing them from collecting and transmitting your personal data. This process is seamless and occurs in the background, ensuring that your browsing experience is not interrupted.</p>
