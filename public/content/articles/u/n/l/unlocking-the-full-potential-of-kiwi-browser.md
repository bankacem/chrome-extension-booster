## Configuring Kiwi Browser Settings for Extensions

To get the most out of your Kiwi browser, you'll need to configure the settings for extensions. This involves navigating to the Kiwi browser settings page, which can be accessed by typing `kiwi://settings/` in the address bar. From here, you can manage your extensions, adjust settings, and even install new ones. For more information on how to find and install the best Chrome extensions, check out our guide on [how to find and download the best Chrome extensions](/blog/how-to-find-and-download-the-best-chrome-extensions-for-a-superior-browsing-experience).

### Enabling and Disabling Extensions

One of the most critical aspects of Kiwi browser settings for extensions is enabling and disabling extensions. This can be done by navigating to the extensions page and toggling the switch next to each extension. You can also use the `kiwi://extensions/` shortcut to access the extensions page directly. For example, if you want to enable the [Light Popup Blocker](/extension/light-popup-blocker) extension, simply toggle the switch to the right.

### Managing Extension Permissions

Another crucial aspect of Kiwi browser settings for extensions is managing extension permissions. This involves granting or denying access to specific features, such as camera or microphone access. You can manage extension permissions by navigating to the extensions page and clicking on the three vertical dots next to each extension. From here, you can adjust the permissions settings to suit your needs. For more information on how to manage extension permissions, check out our guide on [pro security Chrome extensions](/blog/pro-security-chrome-extensions-guide).
