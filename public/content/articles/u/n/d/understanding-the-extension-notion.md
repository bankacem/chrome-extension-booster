<h2 id="what-is-extension-notion">What is Extension Notion?</h2>

<p>The <strong>extension notion</strong> is built around the idea of providing users with a flexible and customizable browsing experience. By installing extensions, users can add new features and functionality to their browser, tailoring it to their specific needs and preferences. This can include anything from <a href="/extension/quick-screenshot-lite" class="text-primary font-medium hover:underline">Quick Screenshot Lite</a> for capturing screenshots to <a href="/extension/auto-dark-mode-switcher" class="text-primary font-medium hover:underline">Auto Dark Mode Switcher</a> for automatically switching between dark and light modes.</p>

<h3>History of Extensions</h3>

<p>The concept of <strong>extension notion</strong> has been around for several years, with early browsers like Mozilla Firefox and Google Chrome introducing support for extensions. Over time, the popularity of extensions has grown, with thousands of developers creating and publishing their own extensions for various browsers.</p>

<h2 id="types-of-extensions">Types of Extensions</h2>

<p>There are numerous types of extensions available, each serving a specific purpose. Some of the most popular types of extensions include:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Productivity extensions, such as <a href="/extension/protab-suspender" class="text-primary font-medium hover:underline">ProTab Suspender</a>, which helps to save memory by suspending inactive tabs</li>

</ul>
