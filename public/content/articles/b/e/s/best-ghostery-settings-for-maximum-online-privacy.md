To get the most out of Ghostery, it's essential to configure the settings correctly. Here are the **best Ghostery settings for maximum online privacy**:

- Enable the "Block All Trackers" option to prevent all tracking technologies from monitoring your online activities.
- Set the "Tracker Blocking" option to "Block" to prevent trackers from loading on websites.
- Enable the "Anti-Tracking" option to prevent websites from using your personal data for targeted advertising.
- Set the "Cookie Blocking" option to "Block" to prevent websites from storing cookies on your device.
