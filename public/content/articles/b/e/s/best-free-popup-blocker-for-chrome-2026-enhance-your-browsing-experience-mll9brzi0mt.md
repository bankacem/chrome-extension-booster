<p>Many people use these terms interchangeably, but they actually serve different purposes. Understanding the distinction is key to choosing the <strong>best popup blocker chrome 2026</strong> has for your specific needs.</p>

<p>An adblocker (like uBlock Origin or AdBlock Plus) is designed to remove *all* advertising from a page. This includes banner ads at the top, video ads on YouTube, and sidebar ads.
