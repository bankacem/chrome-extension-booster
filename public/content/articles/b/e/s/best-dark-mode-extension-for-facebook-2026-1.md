## What is Dark Mode?

Dark mode, also known as night mode, is a display setting that inverts the color scheme of a website or application, replacing the traditional bright background with a darker one. This design choice has gained immense popularity in recent years, and for good reason. **Dark mode** can help reduce eye strain, conserve battery life, and even improve the overall aesthetic of a website.

## Benefits of Dark Mode

The benefits of dark mode are numerous. Some of the most significant advantages include:

- Reduced eye strain: Dark mode can help alleviate eye fatigue caused by prolonged screen time.
- Improved battery life: Dark mode can help conserve battery life on devices with OLED screens.
- Enhanced visual appeal: Dark mode can add a touch of elegance and sophistication to a website's design.
- Increased [productivity](/blog/unlocking-efficiency-the-best-productivity-tools-for-chrome-browser "Unlocking Efficiency: The Best Productivity Tools for Chrome Browser"): Dark mode can help minimize distractions and improve focus.
