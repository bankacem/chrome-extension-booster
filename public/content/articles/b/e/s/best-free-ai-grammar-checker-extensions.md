## How We Picked the Best Free AI Grammar Checker Extensions

"Free" claims deserve scrutiny, so every tool in this list had to clear four bars:

1. **Actually usable without paying** — not a three-day trial wearing a free costume.
2. **Real-time correction inside the page** — no copy-paste round trips to another tab. Every candidate installs through the Chrome Web Store, and the <a href="https://support.google.com/chrome_webstore/answer/2664769?hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Google Web Store Help guide on installing and managing extensions</a> is worth a skim before granting any checker access to the sites where you write.
3. **Transparent data handling** — a readable privacy policy, ideally local processing or anonymization before anything leaves the browser.
4. **Modest resource footprint** — a checker that idles under 50 MB and doesn't spawn a small farm of background processes. If you've ever wondered why Chrome balloons with helper processes, our explainer on [why Chrome opens so many processes](/blog/why-does-chrome-open-so-many-processes) shows why extension architecture matters here.

## 🪶 #1: Plume AI — The Privacy-First Open Source Option

**Free (with API key) | Open source | Updated April 2026 | Chrome & Firefox**

Plume AI was built because the developer was "tired of the 'context switch' — opening a new tab, pasting text, asking for a grammar check, and pasting it back."

### What Makes It Special

**You own the brain:** No monthly subscriptions. Connect your own API key (Gemini, Claude, OpenAI, Mistral) or run it **100% locally with Ollama**.

**Privacy isn't an afterthought:** Your text is anonymized (emails/phones stripped) before it ever hits an API. The original text is restored after the check.

**Native explanations:** It explains grammar mistakes in *your* native language, even if you're writing in another one.

### Key Features

#### Grammar Check

Click the **✦** trigger button that appears when you focus any text field — or press **`Ctrl+Shift+G`** to open the panel instantly (a standard options-page shortcut of the kind documented in the <a href="https://developer.chrome.com/docs/extensions/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Chrome for Developers extension documentation</a>). The extension analyses your text and highlights every grammar, spelling, and word-choice error inline. Each highlight shows the suggested fix and a short explanation. Hit **Fix all** to apply every correction at once.

**Text language support:** English (US/UK), French, German, Spanish, Dutch — or **auto-detect**.

#### Interface Language (i18n)

The extension UI and error explanations adapt to your chosen interface language — independently of the language you are writing in. Set your native language once; the AI will explain grammar mistakes in that language even if the text you are correcting is in a different one.

**Supported interface languages:** English, English (UK), French, German, Spanish, Dutch, Portuguese (PT), Portuguese (BR), Chinese Simplified, **Arabic**, **Persian**, Japanese.

#### AI Rewrite

Hit **✦ Polish** to let the AI rewrite your full text (or just the selected portion) for clarity and natural flow. Review the suggestion, then accept or dismiss.

#### Tone Presets

Four one-click tone pills:

| Pill | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| **Shorter** | Cuts filler, keeps the meaning |
| **Formal** | Professional tone |
| **Direct** | Removes hedging and filler phrases |
| **Technical** | Precise, domain-appropriate language |

#### Translation

Translate the field's content into any supported language in one click — right from the panel, without leaving the page.

#### Accessibility

Built to WCAG 2.1 AA:

- Full keyboard navigation
- Screen reader support (`aria-live` announcements)
- Focus-visible indicators
- 4.5:1 minimum contrast ratio
- Respects OS-level reduced motion preference

### The Catch

- Local Ollama setup requires technical knowledge

### Verdict

**The best choice for privacy-conscious users and multilingual writers.** The ability to explain grammar mistakes in Arabic while you write in English is unique.

Trinka AI is designed specifically for **academic and technical writing** — not general content.

### What Makes It Special

Trinka is a next-generation language and grammar checker for academic writing. Its pitch: "Academic writing needs to be clear, concise, formal, objective, and readable. Trinka... corrects complex grammar errors and goes the extra mile by enhancing your writing for vocabulary, tone, and conciseness."

### Key Features

- **Corrects complex grammar errors** unique to academic writing that other tools don't find
- **Enhances vocabulary, tone, and conciseness**
- **Real-time editing** as you type
- **Add to dictionary** — Add your own words to "My Dictionary"
- **Corrects biased or vague language**

### Where It Works

- Academic writing platforms (Authorea, typeset.io)

### Privacy

Trinka states: "Your data is encrypted with the latest security standards and processed in a highly secure environment. All data transmission between your browser and our servers is secure. Your data is not shared with any third party." Note that this is a cloud-first design — text is processed on their servers, which is exactly what Plume's local mode avoids.

### The Catch

- Designed for academic/technical writing (overkill for casual emails)
- Cloud-based (text sent to their servers)

### Verdict

**Best for students, researchers, and academics.** If you're writing a paper, thesis, or technical document, Trinka catches errors Grammarly misses. ## Frequently Asked Questions

![FAQ about free AI grammar checker extensions in 2026](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1544197150-b99a580bb7a8?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

### Are free AI grammar checkers actually free?

The two featured here are. Trinka AI is fully free for academic and technical use. Plume AI is open source and costs nothing itself — you supply an API key, and Gemini's free tier covers most personal writing. What neither does is lock corrections behind a premium upsell.

### Is my text safe with these extensions?

Plume AI is the strongest option: it anonymizes your text before any API call and can run entirely locally through Ollama, meaning your words never leave your machine. Trinka processes text in the cloud but states that data is encrypted and never shared with third parties. Choose based on your sensitivity threshold.

### Do I need technical skills to set up Plume AI?

Basic copy-paste skills are enough for the API-key route: create a free Gemini key, paste it into the extension's popup, and hit Test connection. Running Ollama locally requires installing software and pulling a model — comfortable territory for developers, optional for everyone else.

### Will a grammar checker slow down Chrome?

Not these two. Plume only activates when you focus a text field, and Trinka weighs in at 435 KiB. Heavy slowdowns usually come from a different culprit entirely — our guide to why Chrome opens so many processes explains how to find it.

### Which is better for non-native English speakers?

Plume AI, and it's not close. Its interface language feature explains English mistakes in twelve languages including Arabic, Persian, and Japanese — far more useful for learning than a silent red underline.
