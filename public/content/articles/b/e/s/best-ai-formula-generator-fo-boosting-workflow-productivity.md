<h2 id="how-it-works">How AI Generates Complex Formulas</h2>
<p>The technology behind the <strong>best AI formula generator for Google Sheets</strong> typically involves Large Language Models (LLMs) trained on millions of lines of spreadsheet data and documentation. When you input a request, the AI performs the following steps:</p>

<h4>1. Intent Analysis</h4>
<p>The AI parses your natural language input to identify the primary goal (e.g., "summing," "filtering," or "lookup"). It identifies the variables mentioned, such as specific dates, currency values, or text strings.</p>

<h4>2. Syntax Mapping</h4>
<p>The tool maps these intents to the specific syntax of Google Sheets. It decides whether an <code>XLOOKUP</code> is more efficient than a <code>VLOOKUP</code> or if a <code>QUERY</code> function is necessary for more complex data retrieval.</p>

<h4>3. Validation</h4>
<p>Before presenting the result, the AI checks for logical consistency, ensuring that the number of arguments provided matches the function's requirements.</p>
