<p>Malicious redirects are unauthorized redirects that occur when you click on a link or visit a website. These redirects can be triggered by malware, viruses, or other types of online threats. They can lead to fake websites that mimic legitimate sites, phishing pages that steal your personal data, or sites that download malware onto your device. Malicious redirects can be particularly dangerous, as they can compromise your online security and put your personal data at risk.</p>

<h3>Types of Malicious Redirects</h3>

<p>There are several types of malicious redirects, including:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Phishing redirects</strong>: These redirects lead to fake websites that mimic legitimate sites, with the goal of stealing your personal data or login credentials.</li>
  <li><strong>Malware redirects</strong>: These redirects download malware onto your device, which can compromise your online security and put your personal data at risk.</li>
  <li><strong>Adware redirects</strong>: These redirects lead to websites that display annoying ads or pop-ups, which can slow down your browser and compromise your online <a href="/blog/best-ad-blocker-for-chrome-android-2026-no-root-boost-your-mobile-browsing-experience-mmb7ls8d81s" class="internal-link" title="Best Ad blocker for Chrome Android 2026 (No Root): Boost Your Mobile Browsing Experience">experience</a>.</li>
</ul>
