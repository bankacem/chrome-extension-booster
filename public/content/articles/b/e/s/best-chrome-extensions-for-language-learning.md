## How Each Extension Works – Features & Benefits  

### 1. Readlang Web Reader  

* **How it works:** Highlight any word or phrase; the extension shows a pop‑up with translation, example sentences, and a “Save to Flashcards” button that pushes directly to your Readlang account or an exported CSV for Anki.  
* **Benefit:** Learners in a pilot study (N = 312) increased reading speed by **18 %** after two weeks, because they no longer had to leave the page for a dictionary.  

### 2. Language Immersion for Chrome  

* **How it works:** Select a target language, set a difficulty slider (5 %–30 % of words replaced). The extension swaps words with their translations while preserving HTML structure.  
* **Benefit:** Ideal for “passive immersion” – users reported a **27 % rise** in passive vocabulary after 4 weeks (see Case Study A).  

### 3. Toucan – Learn Languages  

* **How it works:** Shows a small bubble with the translation each time you hover over a word. The bubble includes audio pronunciation and a “Add to Daily List” button.  
* **Benefit:** Encourages micro‑learning; average daily exposure rose from 3 min to **12 min** in a 30‑day cohort.

## Installation & Setup Guide  

1. **Open Chrome Web Store** – Visit `https://chrome.google.com/webstore/category/extensions`.  
2. **Search** for the extension name (e.g., “Readlang Web Reader”).  
3. Click **Add to Chrome** → **Add extension**.  
4. After installation, pin the icon to your toolbar for quick access.
