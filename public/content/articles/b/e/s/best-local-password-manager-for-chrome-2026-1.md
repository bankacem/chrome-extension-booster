## What is a Local Password Manager?

A local password manager is a type of password manager that stores your encrypted password vault locally on your device, such as your computer or mobile device, rather than on a remote server. This approach offers an additional layer of security and privacy, as your sensitive data is not transmitted over the internet or stored in the cloud where it could potentially be accessed by unauthorized parties.

## Why Do You Need a Local Password Manager?

In today's digital age, the threat of cyberattacks and data breaches is ever-present. Using a local password manager can significantly reduce the risk of your passwords being compromised. Here are a few reasons why you need a local password manager:

- **Enhanced Security:** By storing your passwords locally and encrypting them, you add an extra layer of protection against hackers and data breaches.
- **Privacy:** Your data is not transmitted or stored in the cloud, reducing the risk of unauthorized access.
- **Convenience:** A good local password manager can autofill passwords, generate strong passwords, and provide secure storage for notes and other sensitive information.
