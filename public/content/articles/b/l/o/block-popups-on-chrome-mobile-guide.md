There are several methods to **block popups on Chrome mobile**, including:

### Method 1: Using Chrome's Built-in Popup Blocker

Chrome mobile has a built-in popup blocker that can be enabled in the settings. To access this feature, follow these steps:

1. Open Chrome on your mobile device
2. Tap the three dots on the top right corner
3. Go to Settings
4. Scroll down to the Advanced section
5. Toggle the switch next to "Popups" to enable the blocker

This method is easy to use, but it may not block all types of popups.

### Method 3: Using Browser Settings

You can also **block popups on Chrome mobile** by adjusting your browser settings. For example, you can set Chrome to block all popups by default, or allow popups only from specific websites. To access these settings, follow these steps:

1. Open Chrome on your mobile device
2. Tap the three dots on the top right corner
3. Go to Settings
4. Scroll down to the Advanced section
5. Tap on "Site settings"
6. Toggle the switch next to "Popups" to block or allow popups

This method provides more control over popup blocking, but it may require more effort to configure.
