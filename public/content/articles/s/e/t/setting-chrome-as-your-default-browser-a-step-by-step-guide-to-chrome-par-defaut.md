<h2 id="setting-chrome-as-default-browser">Setting Chrome as Default Browser</h2>

<p>To set Chrome as your default browser, follow these steps:</p>

<ol>
  <li>Open Chrome on your device.</li>
  <li>Click on the three vertical dots in the top right corner of the browser window.</li>
  <li>From the dropdown menu, select "Settings."</li>
  <li>Scroll down to the "Default browser" section.</li>
  <li>Click on the "Make default" button.</li>
</ol>

<p>Alternatively, you can also set <strong>Chrome par defaut</strong> through your device's settings. On Windows, go to "Settings" > "Apps" > "Default apps" and select Chrome as your default browser. On Mac, go to "System Preferences" > "General" and select Chrome as your default web browser.</p>
