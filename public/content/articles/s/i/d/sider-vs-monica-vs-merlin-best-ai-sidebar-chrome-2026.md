## Why This Comparison Exists

The AI sidebar category has a sameness problem. Install Sider, Monica, or Merlin and you'll see the same basic shape: a panel on the side of your browser that chats, summarizes, translates, and writes. The marketing for all three uses the same adjectives. So the real question isn't "which is best" in the abstract — it's which one's trade-offs match what you actually do all day.

Here's the short version before the detail: Sider is the multi-model powerhouse with the biggest user base. Monica is the polished all-rounder with strong writing tools. Merlin leans into research and reading workflows. All three are freemium, all three are actively maintained, and none of them is objectively wrong. The differences are in emphasis, and emphasis is what matters once the novelty wears off.

## Sider: The Multi-Model Workhorse

Sider's defining feature is model choice. Where most AI extensions give you one model behind the curtain, Sider lets you switch between several leading models — GPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, and others — from inside the sidebar. In practice, this matters less for casual questions (any good model answers "summarize this" fine) and more for specialized work: some models write better, some reason better, some handle long documents better. Having the choice in one place is genuinely convenient.

It's also the most popular of the three by a wide margin — millions of users and a 4.9-star rating from over a hundred thousand Chrome Web Store reviews. Popularity isn't quality, but at this scale it means the extension is battle-tested across an enormous range of sites and edge cases, and the developers have the resources to keep it maintained.

The free tier is functional for light use. Paid tiers unlock higher limits and the premium models. If you're the kind of person who has opinions about which AI model is best for which task, Sider is probably already the one you want.

## Monica: The Polished All-Rounder

Monica takes a different approach: instead of emphasizing model choice, it emphasizes workflow polish. The writing tools are the standout — drafting, rewriting, tone adjustment, and translation are all smooth and well-integrated with whatever page you're on. It also handles the standard sidebar duties (summaries, Q&A, image tools) competently.

Where Monica earns its keep is for people whose browser work is mostly composition: emails, social posts, documents, messages. The interface feels designed rather than assembled, and the free tier covers enough to evaluate it properly. Paid plans follow the usual freemium ladder.

If Sider is the tool for model nerds, Monica is the tool for people who just want the thing to work nicely. There's no shame in that preference — it's probably the right one for most users.

## Merlin: The Research Lean

Merlin positions itself closer to the research end of the spectrum. Summaries of articles and videos, document chat, and reading assistance are its strong suits. It handles the "help me understand this thing I'm looking at" workflow well, with a cleaner focus than the Swiss-army-knife approach of its rivals.

It's the least famous of the three, which cuts both ways: less community and fewer third-party guides, but also a product that's had to earn users on merit rather than marketing. The free tier gives you enough room to decide if its reading-first emphasis matches your habits.

## Head-to-Head: What Actually Differs

| | Sider | Monica | Merlin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standout strength | Multiple AI models to choose from | Polished writing tools | Reading and research focus |
| User base | Largest (millions) | Large | Smaller |
| Free tier | Functional for light use | Functional for light use | Functional for light use |

The honest truth: for basic "summarize this page" and "answer my question" tasks, all three are good enough that you'd struggle to tell them apart blind. The differences emerge in sustained use — which model's voice you prefer for drafting, how the sidebar handles your specific document types, whether the writing tools feel native or bolted on.

## The Performance Question

Running any AI sidebar means another extension active in your browser. Individually, these are lightweight — the heavy computation happens on remote servers, not in your tab. The practical advice is the same as with all extensions: don't run two AI sidebars at once. They overlap almost completely, and the redundancy buys you nothing but clutter. Pick one, learn its shortcuts, and uninstall the others.

## Privacy: The Unavoidable Trade-Off

All three send your prompts — and content from pages you ask about — to their servers for processing. That's inherent to how AI extensions work; there's no local-model magic happening in your browser. The practical rule: don't use any AI sidebar on confidential documents, internal tools, or anything you wouldn't paste into a web form. Check each listing's privacy disclosures on the Chrome Web Store before installing, and prefer the one whose data practices you're most comfortable with.

## Verdict: Which Should You Install?

**Pick Sider if:** you care which model answers, you do varied AI tasks, or you just want the most battle-tested option. It's the safest default.

**Pick Monica if:** your browser time is mostly writing — emails, posts, messages — and you value polish over configurability.

**Pick Merlin if:** you're primarily reading — articles, papers, videos — and want a tool tuned for comprehension rather than composition.

**Pick none if:** you only need one AI capability. A dedicated tool (Perplexity for cited answers, Grammarly for writing) will do that one job better than any sidebar does everything. The sidebar format earns its place when you genuinely use three or more of its features weekly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which AI sidebar is best for Chrome in 2026?

There's no universal winner. Sider suits power users who want model choice, Monica suits writers who want polish, and Merlin suits readers. Try the free tier of the one matching your main use case.

### Are these extensions free?

All three are freemium: functional free tiers with daily limits, paid plans for higher usage and premium features. Start free and upgrade only if you hit limits.

### Do AI sidebars slow down Chrome?

Not noticeably on their own — the AI processing happens on remote servers. But don't run two sidebars simultaneously; the overlap wastes resources and clutters your browser.

### Is it safe to use AI sidebars on work documents?

Avoid it for anything confidential. Your prompts and page content are processed on the provider's servers. Check each extension's privacy disclosure on the Chrome Web Store.

### Can I use Sider and Monica together?

Technically yes, practically no. They overlap so completely that running both just duplicates functionality. Pick the one that fits and commit to it.
