## What is Chrome Tab Suspension?

Chrome tab suspension is a feature that allows you to temporarily suspend or freeze inactive tabs in your browser, reducing the amount of system resources they consume. By suspending tabs, you can **save PC resources with Chrome tab suspension** and allocate them to more critical tasks or active tabs. This feature is particularly useful for users who tend to have multiple tabs open at the same time, as it helps to minimize memory usage and prevent browser slowdowns.
