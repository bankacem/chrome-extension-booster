The tab-switching workflow quietly costs more than it looks. Every "open ChatGPT, paste, read answer, switch back" loop breaks your reading context, and a few of those loops per hour adds up to real friction. A side panel extension eliminates the loop entirely:

- **Persistent context**: the AI sits beside the page, so you can ask "summarize this section" without copying anything anywhere.
- **One shortcut away**: most tools bind a hotkey (Alt+G is common) that toggles the panel on any site.
- **Multi-model access**: the best panels put Gemini, ChatGPT, and Claude one click apart, so you can route each question to the model you trust for it.
- **No new browser**: unlike dedicated AI browsers, extensions keep your extensions, profiles, and passwords exactly where they are.

## AI Sidebar — The Minimalist Winner

**Free | Tiny install | Updated March 2026 | Chrome Web Store**

![AI Sidebar minimalist panel — clean desk with a single laptop showing a dark-themed chat sidebar](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1485827404703-89b55fcc595e?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

This tiny extension does one thing and does it perfectly: it puts Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, or NotebookLM in a sleek sidebar.

### Why It's Special

- **Switch models with one click** — click the Gemini tab, then ChatGPT, then Claude, all in the same sidebar.
- **Remembers your preference** — the last model used is saved locally.
- **Resizable sidebar** — drag to your preferred width.
- **Dark minimalist design** — stays completely out of your way.
- **Refresh button** — reload the AI without losing your place.
- **Open in full tab** — when you need more space.

### Privacy

**This is the best part:** the extension explicitly states it "does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It only remembers your AI model preference locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage — nothing is ever sent to external servers."

That's rare for AI extensions.

### The Catch

- You need your own accounts (Google for Gemini/NotebookLM, OpenAI for ChatGPT, Anthropic for Claude).
- Basic sidebar only — no advanced features like prompt saving.

**Verdict: the best choice for 90% of users.** Free, private, tiny, and works with all major AI models.

## Sider — The Power User's Choice

**Freemium | 3.7 million users**

Sider is the most popular side panel extension — and for good reason. It's packed with features.

### Key Features

- **Multi-model chat** — ask one question to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, and DeepSeek simultaneously.
- **Group Chat** — compare answers side by side.
- **Works on PDFs, YouTube, and audio files** — summarize a paper or a two-hour podcast without leaving it.
- **Floating sidebar** follows you as you scroll.
- **Prompt library** built in.

### The Catch

- **Cluttered interface** — lots of features can feel overwhelming.
- **Free tier uses lighter models** (Gemini Flash, Claude Haiku — slower and shallower).
- **Data goes to their servers** — a genuine privacy difference versus local-only tools.

**Verdict: essential for AI researchers or anyone who compares model answers. Overkill for casual users.**

## ChatGPT Atlas — The Browser Alternative

**Free (sidebar) | macOS only | ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo) unlocks agent features**

OpenAI launched its own browser in late 2025. Atlas has ChatGPT built into the foundation — no extension required.

### What Makes Atlas Different

- **Persistent sidebar** activated with Alt+A on every webpage.
- **Understands page context automatically** — no copy-paste needed.
- **Agent Mode** — ChatGPT can control the browser autonomously (find hotels, compare prices, fill forms).
- **Voice control** for hands-free browsing.

### The Catch

- **macOS only** (Apple Silicon + recent macOS).
- Windows and mobile versions are promised but undated.
- Basic sidebar is free, but **Agent Mode requires ChatGPT Plus ($20/month)**.
- **Extension support is basic** — your favorite Chrome extensions might not work properly.
- Early testing found the agent feature slow compared with doing the task yourself.

**Verdict:** if you're on a Mac and don't rely on obscure Chrome extensions, Atlas is a glimpse of where browsing is going. If you need the full Chrome extension ecosystem, stick with Chrome plus a side panel extension.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do ChatGPT side panel chrome extensions cost money?

AI Sidebar is completely free. Sider is freemium — the free tier works but routes to lighter models, with paid plans unlocking premium models and features. ChatGPT Atlas's basic sidebar is free, though its agent features require a ChatGPT Plus subscription.

### Do these extensions work with my existing ChatGPT Plus or Claude account?

Yes. Tools like AI Sidebar log you into your own accounts for each model, so your existing subscriptions and chat history carry over. Nothing about your plan changes — the extension is just a window into the services you already use.

### Are side panel extensions safe for private data?

It varies by tool, and this is the most important differentiator. AI Sidebar states it collects no data and stores preferences locally. Sider processes through its cloud servers. Check each extension's privacy policy before using it on confidential documents, and prefer local-processing tools for sensitive work.

### Can I use multiple AI models in the same sidebar?

Yes. AI Sidebar switches between Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, and NotebookLM with one click. Sider goes further with group chat, sending one prompt to several models simultaneously so you can compare answers side by side.

### Why not just use ChatGPT's own website in a pinned tab?

You can — but a pinned tab still means switching, it won't know which page you're reading, and it can't summarize the PDF or video in front of you. Side panel extensions integrate with page context and remove the switch entirely, which is the entire productivity gain.
