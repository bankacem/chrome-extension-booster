## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a chatgpt search history extension safe to use?

The tools covered here process everything locally in your browser — your conversations are indexed on your own machine and never uploaded to a third-party server. As with any extension, install from the official Chrome Web Store and check the requested permissions before accepting.

### Do these extensions work with Claude, Gemini, and other AI platforms?

LLMnesia supports ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and DeepSeek in a single search. 1Proompt and StylerGPT are ChatGPT-focused. If you split your time across multiple AI platforms, cross-platform search is the deciding feature.

### Will a search history extension slow down my browser?

Indexing runs in the background and the good tools are lightweight — the entire category exists on extensions smaller than most games' loading screens. You may notice a brief indexing pass after first install; after that, day-to-day impact is negligible.

### Can I search conversations after clearing my ChatGPT history?

Only what the extension has already indexed. If you delete conversations from your ChatGPT account, locally stored copies may remain searchable depending on the tool — which is handy for recovery, but also worth knowing if you expected deletion to be complete.

### Are these extensions free?

LLMnesia and 1Proompt offer their core search functionality for free. StylerGPT uses a freemium model where themes, folders, and some organization features sit behind a paid tier. None of them require payment for basic conversation search.
