## 🔍 The Problem with ChatGPT's Native Custom Instructions

OpenAI's built-in Custom Instructions feature has two problems:

1. **Limited to two fields** — "What would you like ChatGPT to know about you?" and "How would you like ChatGPT to respond?"
2. **Global application** — The same instructions apply to every conversation

That's it. For power users, this is painfully insufficient.
