## What Is a ChatGPT Custom Instructions Extension?

![What a ChatGPT custom instructions extension does — user teaching an AI assistant](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1620712943543-bcc4688e7485?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

A ChatGPT custom instructions extension is a browser add-on that expands how you tell the model who you are and how to respond. Native Custom Instructions gives every conversation the same two fields; an extension replaces that ceiling with a full management layer — reusable prompts, fill-in templates, per-context tone, and one-click insertion from the composer.

### The Problem with Native Custom Instructions

OpenAI's built-in feature has two structural limits:

1. **Limited to two fields** — "What would you like ChatGPT to know about you?" and "How would you like ChatGPT to respond?"
2. **Global application** — The same instructions apply to every conversation, whether you're drafting a legal email or brainstorming a birthday party.

For power users who switch between work and personal contexts all day, this is painfully insufficient. Extensions fix both limits: instructions become per-prompt, per-folder, and fillable at insert time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

![ChatGPT custom instructions extension FAQ — personalization settings concept](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1535378917042-10a22c95931a?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

### Do I still need native Custom Instructions if I install an extension?

Yes, keep the native fields for truly global preferences — language, disclaimers, formatting defaults. The extension handles everything context-specific: reusable prompts, templates with variables, and per-folder instructions. The two layers work together rather than competing.

### Is StylerGPT free?

StylerGPT is freemium. Basic theming and navigation are free, while the Prompt Manager, variables, and Prompt Enhancer sit behind a paid plan with a 7-day free trial. If you only need style-level control, the free Plume AI route with your own API key is the zero-cost alternative.

### Are custom instructions sent to OpenAI automatically?

Native and inserted instructions become part of your prompt, so yes — anything inserted is processed by the model like any other text. The extension itself stores your library locally in the browser; it doesn't upload your prompt library to a third party.

### Can I use prompt variables without knowing any code?

Yes. Variables are just bracketed placeholders like [CLIENT_NAME]. When you insert the prompt, the extension prompts you to fill each field through a simple form — no scripting or technical knowledge required.

### Will an extension slow ChatGPT down?

Modern options are lightweight in practice. StylerGPT is the largest at 5.26MiB, but its tools activate on the ChatGPT page only. If you notice lag, disable features you don't use in the extension's settings panel.

### What happens to my prompt library if I switch computers?

Because libraries are stored locally, they don't sync automatically. Export your prompts before migrating, or re-import them on the new machine — and keep an occasional backup so a browser reset doesn't erase months of template work.
