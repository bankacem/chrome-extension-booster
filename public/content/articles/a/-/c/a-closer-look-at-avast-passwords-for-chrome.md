## What is Extension Avast Password?

The **extension Avast password** is a browser extension designed to help users manage their passwords securely and efficiently. Developed by Avast, a renowned cybersecurity company, this extension offers a range of features aimed at simplifying password management, including:

- Password generation: Create strong, unique passwords for each of your online accounts.
- Password storage: Store all your passwords in a secure, encrypted vault.
- Auto-fill: Automatically fill in login credentials for your online accounts.
- Password sharing: Share passwords with trusted individuals or teams.
