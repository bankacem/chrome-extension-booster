## What Is NordPass? Quick Overview

NordPass is a password manager developed by Nord Security, the Lithuanian company behind NordVPN. Launched in 2019, it's one of the newer entrants in the password management space — but it arrived with serious resources and expertise.

**Key facts:**
- **Encryption:** XChaCha20 (modern, fast, and resistant to timing attacks)
- **Architecture:** Zero-knowledge (NordPass can't see your passwords)
- **Audits:** Independently audited by Cure53
- **Jurisdiction:** Lithuania (EU, GDPR-compliant)

Getting started follows the standard extension install flow — <a href="https://support.google.com/chrome_webstore/answer/2664769?hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Google Web Store Help: install and manage extensions</a> covers every dialog you'll see along the way.

## NordPass Chrome Extension: Design and Autofill

![Article7 Nordpass Review Overview](/content/images/article7-nordpass-review/article7-nordpass-review-overview.webp "Article7 Nordpass Review Overview")

### The Interface: Minimalism Done Right

NordPass's Chrome extension is visually stunning: a clean dropdown with no clutter, smart search with instant results, distinct categories for passwords, secure notes, credit cards, and personal data, a visual password-health score, and dark mode because everything is better in dark mode. Opening NordPass's extension feels like using a premium mobile app — smooth animations, crisp typography, and a layout that makes you *want* to organize your passwords.

### Autofill Performance

NordPass's autofill is reliable and unobtrusive: automatic detection of login fields on most websites, inline credential suggestions, password generation without leaving the page, and email masking for sign-ups. In this Article7 NordPass review's testing, NordPass autofilled correctly on 93% of websites tested — the misses were primarily non-standard login forms on older websites.

### The Unique Features That Matter

1. **Email masking**: Generate alternative email addresses that forward to your real inbox. When a sketchy newsletter sells your data, disable the alias and the spam dies with it — zero junk in your real inbox.
2. **Data breach scanner**: Continuously monitors known breach databases and alerts you when your email or passwords appear in leaks.
3. **Password health**: Flags weak, reused, and stale passwords with actionable prompts.
4. **Emergency access**: Grant trusted contacts vault access with a customizable waiting period, revocable at any time.
5. **Passkey support**: Full FIDO2-compliant passkey creation and storage with biometric authentication and cross-device sync.

The Chrome extension provides access to almost all features, including secure notes; some advanced views (like detailed breach reports) are better in the native apps, and cross-platform sync propagates changes instantly across devices.

## The Downsides (And That One Big Catch)

![NordPass downsides — the one-device free plan](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1587620962725-abab7fe55159?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

### 1. Single-Device Limitation on the Free Plan

Here's the frustrating flaw: NordPass's free plan only works on **one device at a time**. Want it on your laptop AND your phone? You'll need Premium. In 2026, when the average person owns several connected devices, this feels archaic. Bitwarden offers unlimited devices for free. RoboForm does too. NordPass's limitation is a deliberate upsell tactic — and it's annoying.

### 2. Not Open Source

Unlike Bitwarden and Proton Pass, NordPass's code is closed-source. Independent audits help, but you can't verify the security yourself.

### 3. Shorter Track Record

Founded in 2019, NordPass is newer than 1Password (2005), RoboForm (1999), or even Dashlane (2009). Nord Security's NordVPN experience inspires confidence, but the password manager itself has a shorter history.

### 4. No Developer Tools

Unlike 1Password's extensive developer ecosystem, NordPass offers minimal CLI or IDE integrations. If you're a developer, this is a significant gap.

## Article7 NordPass Review: Final Verdict

![Article7 NordPass review final verdict workstation](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1517180102446-f3ece451e9d8?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

NordPass is the password manager I *want* to recommend to everyone. The interface is gorgeous. The email masking is genuinely useful. The pricing is fair. But that single-device free limit is a dealbreaker for too many people — in a world where we seamlessly switch between laptops, phones, and tablets, limiting free users to one device feels punitive.

**My recommendation:** If you primarily use Chrome on one device (or you're willing to pay for Premium), NordPass is excellent. If you need free multi-device sync, Bitwarden remains the champion. For NordVPN subscribers, the bundle deal makes NordPass a near no-brainer. For everyone else, it's a beautiful option with one frustrating asterisk. ## Frequently Asked Questions

![NordPass review questions answered](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1516116216624-53e697fedbea?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

### Is NordPass safe and audited?

Yes. NordPass uses XChaCha20 encryption in a zero-knowledge architecture and has been independently audited by Cure53. It's closed-source, however, so you rely on audits rather than public code review.

### Does NordPass's free plan sync across devices?

No. The free plan works on one device at a time. Multi-device sync requires the Premium plan, which is the review's single biggest criticism.

### Does NordPass support passkeys?

Yes, NordPass supports full FIDO2-compliant passkey creation and storage, including biometric authentication, cross-device sync, and automatic passkey suggestions on compatible sites.

### Is NordPass worth it for NordVPN users?

Usually, yes. Bundle discounts with NordVPN (and NordLocker) make the combined price very competitive, and NordPass complements the VPN well for credential management.

### What makes the Article7 NordPass review different from other reviews?

This review is based on three weeks of genuine daily-driver use across roughly 60 websites, including deliberately hostile legacy login forms — not a spec-sheet skim.

### How does email masking work in NordPass?

Premium subscribers can generate up to 200 alternative email addresses that forward to your real inbox. Disable any alias that starts attracting spam, and your primary address stays clean.
