## Headline Formulas That Work Across Every Industry

### The 10 Proven Headline Templates

**1. The How-To Formula**
```
How to [Achieve Desired Result] in [Timeframe] Without [Common Obstacle]
```
Example: *"How to Write a Bestselling Book in 90 Days Without Quitting Your Job"*

**2. The Listicle Formula**
```
[Number] [Adjective] Ways to [Achieve Result] That [Surprising Benefit]
```
Example: *"17 Brilliant Ways to Save Money on Groceries That Actually Work"*

**3. The Secret Formula**
```
The [Adjective] Secret [Target Audience] Use to [Achieve Result]
```
Example: *"The Counterintuitive Secret Top Salespeople Use to Close 90% of Deals"*

**4. The Mistake Formula**
```
[Number] [Topic] Mistakes You're Making (And How to Fix Them)
```
Example: *"7 SEO Mistakes You're Making That Are Killing Your Rankings"*

**5. The Question Formula**
```
Are You [Undesirable State]? Here's Why (And How to [Desired State])
```
Example: *"Are You Constantly Exhausted? Here's Why (And How to Fix It)"*

**6. The Comparison Formula**
```
[Thing A] vs. [Thing B]: Which [Result] Better?
```
Example: *"Notion vs. Obsidian: Which Note-Taking App Actually Boosts Productivity?"*

**7. The Ultimate Guide Formula**
```
The Ultimate Guide to [Topic]: [Specific Promise]
```
Example: *"The Ultimate Guide to Remote Work: 47 Tools, 12 Strategies, Zero Burnout"*

**8. The Case Study Formula**
```
How [Company/Person] [Achieved Result] in [Timeframe] Using [Method]
```
Example: *"How Buffer Grew to 100K Users in 9 Months Using Only Content Marketing"*

**9. The Contrarian Formula**
```
Why [Common Belief] Is Wrong (And What [Target Audience] Should Do Instead)
```
Example: *"Why 'Follow Your Passion' Is Terrible Advice (And What to Do Instead)"*

**10. The Urgency Formula**
```
[Time-Sensitive Element]: [Consequence of Inaction] + [Benefit of Action]
```
Example: *"Google's March 2026 Update: Why Your Site Will Lose 40% of Traffic (And the Fix)"*

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can AI really write better headlines than humans?

**Yes, for data-driven optimization.** AI excels at: - Analyzing millions of headline patterns
- Identifying emotional triggers that work
- Optimizing for specific platforms
- A/B testing at scale

**Humans still win at: **
- Understanding nuanced brand voice
- Cultural context and timing
- Controversial or sensitive topics
- Creative wordplay and puns

**Best approach: ** AI generates options, humans select and refine.

### Will AI headlines sound clickbait-y?

**Only if you let them.** Quality AI tools like ExtensionTo balance: - Curiosity gap (enough to click, not so much it's misleading)
- Promise delivery (headline matches content)
- Emotional honesty (no fake urgency)

**The "clickbait filter"** scores headlines on promise accuracy and flags potentially misleading options.

### How do I optimize headlines for voice search?

**Voice search headlines are different: **

- Optimize for featured snippets
