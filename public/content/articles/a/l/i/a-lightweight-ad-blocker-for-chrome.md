## What is a Lightweight Ad Blocker?

A **lightweight ad blocker Chrome** extension is a type of software designed to block unwanted ads and popups while browsing the internet. Unlike traditional ad blockers, lightweight ad blockers are optimized for [performance](/blog/unlocking-peak-performance-browser-optimization-extensions) and use minimal system resources, making them ideal for users who want to enjoy a seamless browsing experience without compromising their device's performance.

### How Does a Lightweight Ad Blocker Work?

A **lightweight ad blocker Chrome** extension works by filtering out unwanted ads and popups from web pages. It uses a combination of algorithms and blacklists to identify and block ads, ensuring that only relevant content is displayed to the user. This not only improves the overall browsing experience but also helps to reduce the risk of malware and other online threats.
