<h2 id="how-do-anti-captcha-chrome-extensions-work">How do Anti Captcha Chrome Extensions Work?</h2>

<p>The inner workings of anti captcha Chrome extensions vary depending on their approach to CAPTCHA solving. Some extensions rely on optical character recognition (OCR) technology to decipher visual CAPTCHAs, while others use machine learning models to predict the correct solution. In some cases, extensions may even crowdsource CAPTCHA solutions from their user base.</p>

<p>For instance, an anti captcha Chrome extension might use a combination of computer vision and natural language processing to identify and solve CAPTCHAs. This process typically involves the following steps:</p>

<ol>
  <li>Detecting the presence of a CAPTCHA on a webpage</li>
  <li>Extracting the CAPTCHA image or audio challenge</li>
  <li>Applying OCR or machine learning algorithms to solve the CAPTCHA</li>
  <li>Submitting the solution to the website</li>
</ol>
