## How Ad Blockers Work

Ad blockers work by filtering out ads from web pages, including YouTube. They use various techniques, such as blocking ad scripts, hiding ad elements, or redirecting ad requests. By installing an **ad blocker that works on YouTube Chrome**, you can enjoy ad-free video streaming, reducing distractions and improving your overall browsing experience.

### Types of Ad Blockers

There are two primary types of ad blockers: [browser extensions](/blog/the-elite-stack-essential-chrome-extensions-for-work-pro-environments) and standalone applications. Browser extensions, like our [Light Popup Blocker](/extension/light-popup-blocker), are easy to install and provide a convenient way to block ads on YouTube and other websites. ## Features to Look for in a YouTube Ad Blocker
