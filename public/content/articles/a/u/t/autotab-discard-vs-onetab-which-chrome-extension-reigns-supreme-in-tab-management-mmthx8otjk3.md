<h2 id="autotab-discard-vs-onetab">Autotab Discard vs Onetab: An Overview</h2>

<p>Both Autotab Discard and Onetab are designed to help users manage their tabs more efficiently. Autotab Discard automatically discards tabs that are not in use, while Onetab consolidates all tabs into a single list. But what are the key differences between these two extensions? Let's take a closer look at their features and benefits.</p>

<h3 id="features-of-autotab-discard">Features of Autotab Discard</h3>

<p>Autotab Discard offers a range of features that make it an attractive option for users. Some of its key features include: </p>
<ul>
  <li>Automatic tab discard: Autotab Discard automatically discards tabs that are not in use, freeing up memory and reducing clutter.</li>
  <li>Customizable settings: Users can customize the settings to discard tabs after a specified period of inactivity.</li>
  <li>Whitelist: Autotab Discard allows users to whitelist specific tabs or websites that they want to keep open.</li>
</ul>

<h3 id="features-of-onetab">Features of Onetab</h3>

<p>Onetab, on the other hand, offers a unique approach to tab management. Some of its key features include: </p>
<ul>
  <li>Tab consolidation: Onetab consolidates all tabs into a single list, making it easy to manage and access tabs.</li>
  <li>Search functionality: Onetab allows users to search for specific tabs, making it easy to find what they need.</li>
  <li>Customizable layout: Users can customize the layout of their tab list to suit their preferences.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="comparison-of-autotab-discard-and-onetab">Comparison of Autotab Discard and Onetab</h2>
