<p>Google Tag Manager is a free tool that allows you to manage and deploy marketing and analytics tags (snippets of code or tracking pixels) on your website or mobile app. With Google Tag Manager, you can easily add, edit, and remove tags without having to modify your website's code. This makes it an essential tool for marketers, developers, and analysts who want to track website behavior, conversions, and other key metrics.</p>
