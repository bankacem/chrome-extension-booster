## How to Install the Google Translate Extension to Chrome

![Google Translate Extension To Chrome 6 Overview](/content/images/google-translate-extension-to-chrome-6/google-translate-extension-to-chrome-6-overview.webp "Google Translate Extension To Chrome 6 Overview")


Installing the **Google Translate extension to Chrome** is a breeze. Simply follow these steps:

1. Open the [Chrome Web Store](/blog/chrome-web-store-guide "Unlocking the Power of the Chrome Web Store: A Comprehensive Guide") and search for "Google Translate"
2. Click the "Add to Chrome" button
3. Confirm the installation by clicking "Add extension"

Once installed, the Google Translate extension will appear in your Chrome toolbar, ready to use.
