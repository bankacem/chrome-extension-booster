## [Introduction](/blog/extension-chrome-presearch-14 "Unlock the Power of Private Search: Introduction to Extension Chrome Presearch") to Ghostery and Privacy Badger

Both Ghostery and Privacy Badger are designed to block trackers, scripts, and other malicious elements that compromise our online privacy. However, they differ in their approach, features, and functionality. **Ghostery** is a well-established extension that uses a blocklist approach to identify and block trackers, while **Privacy Badger** employs a more advanced, AI-powered system to detect and block trackers.

### Key Features of Ghostery

Ghostery offers a range of features that make it a popular choice among users. Some of its key features include:

- Blocklist approach to identify and block trackers
- Customizable blocking options for advanced users
- Tracker library with detailed information on blocked trackers
- Anti-tracking features to prevent fingerprinting and other tracking methods

### Key Features of Privacy Badger

Privacy Badger, on the other hand, boasts an impressive set of features that set it apart from Ghostery. Some of its key features include:

- AI-powered system to detect and block trackers
- Automatic blocking of trackers based on user behavior
- Advanced fingerprinting protection to prevent tracking
- Integration with other privacy-focused tools and extensions
