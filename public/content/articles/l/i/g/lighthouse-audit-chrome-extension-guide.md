## What is Lighthouse?

Lighthouse is an open-source, automated tool developed by Google that helps you improve the quality of your website by providing a comprehensive audit of its performance, accessibility, and best practices. It's available as a Chrome extension, making it easy to use and access. With Lighthouse, you can identify areas for improvement and optimize your website for better user experience, search engine rankings, and overall performance.

## How to Use Lighthouse

![Lighthouse Audit Chrome Extension Guide Overview](/content/images/lighthouse-audit-chrome-extension-guide/lighthouse-audit-chrome-extension-guide-overview.webp "Lighthouse Audit Chrome Extension Guide Overview")


Using Lighthouse is straightforward. Simply install the Chrome extension, navigate to the website you want to audit, and click on the Lighthouse icon in your browser toolbar. Then, select the types of audits you want to run (e.g., performance, accessibility, best practices) and click on the "Generate Report" button. Lighthouse will then analyze your website and provide a detailed report [highlighting](/blog/extension-surligneur-chrome-10 "Unlock the Power of Highlighting with the Best Extension Surligneur Chrome") areas for improvement.

## Lighthouse Audit Categories

Lighthouse provides a comprehensive audit of your website across several categories, including:

- Performance: Measures how quickly your website loads and responds to user interactions.
- Accessibility: Evaluates how accessible your website is to users with disabilities.
- Best Practices: Assesses your website's adherence to web [development](/blog/top-chrome-devtools-tips-for-mobile "Unlock the Power of Mobile Development: Top Chrome DevTools Tips for Mobile") best practices, such as security, SEO, and mobile-friendliness.
- SEO: Analyzes your website's search engine optimization, including metadata, headings, and content quality.
- Progressive Web App (PWA): Evaluates your website's ability to provide a native app-like experience to users.
