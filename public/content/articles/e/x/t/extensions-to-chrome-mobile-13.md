<h2 id="how-to-install-extensions-on-chrome-mobile">How to Install Extensions on Chrome Mobile</h2>

<p>Installing <strong>extensions to Chrome mobile</strong> is a straightforward process. Here's a step-by-step guide to get you started:</p>

<ol>
  <li>Open the Chrome browser on your mobile device.</li>
  <li>Tap the three vertical dots in the top right corner of the screen.</li>
  <li>Tap "Settings" from the dropdown menu.</li>
  <li>Scroll down and tap "Extensions" or "Desktop site" to enable extension installation.</li>
