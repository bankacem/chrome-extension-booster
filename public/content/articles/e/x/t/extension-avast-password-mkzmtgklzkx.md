<h2 id="what-is-extension-avast-password">What is Extension Avast Password?</h2>

<p>The <strong>extension Avast password</strong> is a browser extension designed to help users manage their passwords securely and efficiently. Developed by Avast, a renowned cybersecurity company, this extension offers a range of features aimed at simplifying password management, including:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Password generation: Create strong, unique passwords for each of your online accounts.</li>
  <li>Password storage: Store all your passwords in a secure, encrypted vault.</li>
  <li>Auto-fill: Automatically fill in login credentials for your online accounts.</li>
  <li>Password sharing: Share passwords with trusted individuals or teams.</li>
</ul>
