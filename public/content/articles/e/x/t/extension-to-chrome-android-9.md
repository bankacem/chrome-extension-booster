## Installing Extensions on Chrome Android

Installing an extension to Chrome Android is a straightforward process. Here's a step-by-step guide:

1. Open the Google Chrome browser on your Android device.
2. Tap the three dots in the top-right corner of the screen.
3. Tap "Settings" from the drop-down menu.
4. Scroll down to the "Advanced" section.
5. Tap "Extensions" to access the extensions menu.
6. Tap "Chrome Web Store" to browse available extensions.
7. Search for the extension you want to install and tap "Add to Chrome" to install it.

Some popular extensions that you can install on your Chrome Android browser include [Quick Screenshot Lite](/extension/quick-screenshot-lite) for capturing screenshots and [Auto Dark Mode Switcher](/extension/auto-dark-mode-switcher) for [automatically](/blog/how-to-hibernate-inactive-tabs-automatically-6 "How to Hibernate Inactive Tabs Automatically: The Ultimate Guide to a Faster Browser") switching between dark and light modes.
