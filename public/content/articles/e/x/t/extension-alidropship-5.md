Before we dive in, it's essential to understand what AliDropship is and how it works. AliDropship is a popular plugin designed for WooCommerce, a widely-used ecommerce platform for WordPress. The **extension alidropship** allows you to import products from AliExpress, a leading online wholesale marketplace, and sell them on your own website. This eliminates the need to hold any inventory, as products are shipped directly from the supplier to the customer.

## Key Features of the Extension AliDropship

The **extension alidropship** offers a range of features that make it an attractive solution for dropshippers. Some of the key benefits include:

- **Product Import:** Easily import products from AliExpress, including product descriptions, images, and prices.
- **Auto-Ordering:** Automatically place orders on AliExpress when a customer makes a purchase on your website.
- **Price Automation:** Set prices for your products and automatically update them based on changes in the supplier's pricing.
- **Shipping Integration:** Track shipments and provide customers with updates on the status of their orders.
