<p>Popups are small windows that appear on your screen, usually with advertisements, offers, or alerts. They can be triggered by visiting certain websites, clicking on links, or even by malware. The primary goal of a <strong>Chrome extension to stop popups</strong> is to block these unwanted windows, ensuring your browsing experience remains uninterrupted.</p>
