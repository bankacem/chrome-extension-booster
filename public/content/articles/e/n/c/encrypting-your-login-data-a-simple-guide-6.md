<p>Encryption is the process of converting plaintext data into unreadable ciphertext to protect it from unauthorized access. In the context of login data, encryption ensures that your usernames, passwords, and other sensitive information are scrambled and cannot be deciphered by hackers or malicious actors. <strong>Encrypting your login data: A simple guide</strong> will walk you through the process of using encryption to safeguard your online accounts.</p>

<h3>Types of encryption</h3>

<p>There are several types of encryption algorithms, including symmetric and asymmetric encryption. Symmetric encryption uses the same key for both encryption and decryption, while asymmetric encryption uses a pair of keys: a public key for encryption and a private key for decryption.
