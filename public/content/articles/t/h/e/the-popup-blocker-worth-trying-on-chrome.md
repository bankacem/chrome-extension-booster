## What is a Popup Blocker?

A popup blocker is a software or extension that prevents unwanted popups and ads from appearing on your screen. These blockers work by identifying and blocking scripts that generate popups, ensuring that you can browse the web without interruptions. With a **best popup blocker Chrome extension**, you can enjoy a faster and more secure browsing experience, free from annoying popups and ads.

### How Do Popup Blockers Work?

Popup blockers use various techniques to identify and block popups. Some common methods include:

- Filtering scripts: Popup blockers can filter out scripts that generate popups, preventing them from loading on your browser.
- Blocking URLs: Popup blockers can block specific URLs that are known to generate popups, ensuring that you don't encounter them while browsing.
- Using AI: Some advanced popup blockers use artificial [intelligence](/blog/best-ai-formula-generator-for-google-sheets-1 "Best AI Formula Generator for Google Sheets: Enhancing Productivity with Intelligence") to identify and block popups, providing a more effective and efficient solution.
