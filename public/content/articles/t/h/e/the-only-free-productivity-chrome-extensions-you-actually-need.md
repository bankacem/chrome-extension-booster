<p>The Tab Tamers (Because You Have a Hoarding Problem)</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --> <!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>Context switching is the enemy. Every time you jump between tabs, your brain loses focus. It takes about 23 minutes to get back into deep work after an interruption. If you have 50 tabs open, you aren't multitasking; you're just drowning.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --> <!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} --></p>
<ol class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>OneTab<br />This is the nuclear option. And it is glorious. When you feel that panic rising because you can't see your favicons anymore, you click the OneTab icon.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ol>
<p><!-- /wp:list --> <!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>Boom.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --> <!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>All your open tabs are swept into a single list on a new page. It saves up to 95% of your memory instantly. Your computer stops wheezing. It&rsquo;s not just about saving RAM; it&rsquo;s a psychological reset. You can restore them one by one or all at once. I use this at the end of every workday to clear the decks for the next morning.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --> <!-- wp:list {"ordered":true,"start":2} --></p>
<ol class="wp-block-list" start="2"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Workona<br />If OneTab is a broom, Workona is a filing cabinet. It turns your browser into an OS. You can create "workspaces" for different projects. One for "Client A," one for "Research," one for "Personal." <p>The "Shut Up and Work" Tools</p>

<ol class="wp-block-list" start="3"><!-- wp:list-item -->

<!-- /wp:list-item --></ol>
<p><!-- /wp:list --> <!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>The "Nuclear Option" feature is particularly sadistic. It prevents you from changing the settings once you've locked them in. It forces you to work. It&rsquo;s aggressive, annoying, and absolutely necessary.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --> <!-- wp:list {"ordered":true,"start":4} --></p>
<ol class="wp-block-list" start="4"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>News Feed Eradicator<br />Social media is useful for networking. The infinite scroll is not. This extension replaces your entire feed on Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, and YouTube with a nice, boring inspirational quote.</li>

<p>How to Install Without Breaking Your Browser</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --> <!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>A word of caution before you go on a download spree. Productivity chrome extensions free or paid, all consume resources.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --> <!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>Here is my rule of three: Never have more than three "active" background extensions running at once (like ad blockers or grammar checkers). Tools like GoFullPage or OneTab only run when you click them, so you can stack those up. But keep the background processes lean. If your browser starts lagging, audit your extensions. Disable what you aren't using this week.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --> <!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>Your browser is your cockpit. Keep it clean, keep it sharp, and get back to work.</p>
