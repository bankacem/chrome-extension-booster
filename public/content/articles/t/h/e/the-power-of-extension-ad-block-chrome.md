<h2 id="what-is-an-ad-blocker">What is an Ad Blocker?</h2>
<p>An ad blocker is a type of software or <strong>extension ad block Chrome</strong> tool that prevents ads from being displayed on web pages. By blocking ads, you can enjoy a faster browsing experience, reduce distractions, and even help protect your device from malware and other online threats.</p>

<h3>How Ad Blockers Work</h3>
<p>Ad blockers work by using a combination of algorithms and filters to identify and block ads on web pages. They can block ads based on their format, such as images or videos, or based on their source, such as specific ad networks or domains.</p>

<p>There are many benefits to using an <strong>extension ad block Chrome</strong> tool, including:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Faster browsing experience: By blocking ads, you can reduce the amount of data being loaded on web pages, resulting in faster load times.</li>

  <li>Improved security: Ad blockers can help protect your device from malware and other online threats by blocking malicious ads.</li>
