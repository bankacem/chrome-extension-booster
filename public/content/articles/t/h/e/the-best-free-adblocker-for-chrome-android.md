## What is an Adblocker?

An adblocker is a software or browser extension that blocks unwanted ads from appearing on your device. Adblockers can block various types of ads, including pop-ups, banners, and video ads. They work by filtering out ads from websites, preventing them from loading on your device. This not only improves your browsing experience but also helps protect your device from malware and other security threats.

### Types of Adblockers

There are several types of adblockers available, including:

- [Browser](/blog/the-elite-stack-essential-chrome-extensions-for-work-pro-environments "The Elite Stack: Essential Chrome Extensions for Work Pro Environments") [extensions](/blog/effortless-image-downloading-bulk-image-downloader-chrome-extensions "Effortless Image Downloading: A Comprehensive Guide to Bulk Image Downloader Chrome Extensions"): These are add-ons that can be installed on your browser to block ads.

- Network-level adblockers: These block ads at the network level, preventing them from reaching your device.
