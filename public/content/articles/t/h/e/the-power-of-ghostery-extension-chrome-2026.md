Ghostery is a privacy-focused browser extension available for Chrome, among other browsers, that helps users detect and block tracking technologies used by websites. ### How Ghostery Works

Upon installation, the Ghostery extension Chrome 2026 begins to scan the websites you visit for tracking scripts. These scripts can be from a variety of sources, including advertisers, analytics services, and social media platforms. Once identified, Ghostery blocks these scripts, preventing them from collecting and transmitting your personal data.
