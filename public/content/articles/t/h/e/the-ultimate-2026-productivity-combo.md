Google has officially retired the legacy web store interface. The **only** active and authorized URL format in 2026 is:

### Step-by-Step Installation Layout:
1. Open your desktop Google Chrome browser.
2. Navigate directly to `chromewebstore.google.com`.
3. Use the top-left search input field and type your preferred utility (e.g., `Sider AI sidebar`).

5. Approve the installation, click the jigsaw puzzle icon on your toolbar, and click the **Pin** icon next to the tool.

## Frequently Asked Questions (Summarizers)

### Is there a completely free AI summarizer with zero daily usage limits?
No. Processing large-scale tokens through advanced LLMs incurs real server infrastructure costs. The most functional free tier in 2026 remains **Sider AI with 30 free operations daily**. For unlimited local workflows, users typically plug in their own personal API keys.

### Can it summarize local PDF documents opened inside a Chrome tab?
Yes. Both Sider and Brisk fully support PDF processing. However, you must first navigate to `chrome://extensions`, click details on the target extension, and manually switch on the **"Allow access to file URLs"** safety toggle.

### Will running these summary scripts constantly drag down my browser's performance?
Not if you run them individually. Avoid keeping multiple automated overlay tools active simultaneously to prevent background memory overhead and DOM conflicts.

## Frequently Asked Questions (Gemini Tools)

### Can I utilize third-party Gemini extensions alongside Chrome's native Alt+G panel?
Yes. They operate on entirely separate rendering threads. You can keep Chrome’s native tool open on the right side to track your calendar, while using Sider or Harpa on the left side to benchmark code or automate web data tasks.

### Do custom prompt shortcuts built inside extensions sync directly into Google's main Gemini web interface?
No. External extension frameworks store your custom prompt structures, shortcut libraries, and historical data logs in their own sandboxed local storage networks. Your configurations will not carry over if you log into the official Gemini web app on a mobile device or a different browser profile.
