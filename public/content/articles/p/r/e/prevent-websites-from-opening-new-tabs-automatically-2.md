## What are Automatically Opening Tabs?

Automatically opening tabs are new tabs that open in your browser without your consent or interaction. These tabs can be triggered by various factors, including malicious scripts, ads, or website design. When a website opens a new tab automatically, it can be distracting and disrupt your browsing experience.

## Why do Websites Open New Tabs Automatically?

![Prevent Websites From Opening New Tabs Automatically 2 Overview](/content/images/prevent-websites-from-opening-new-tabs-automatically-2/prevent-websites-from-opening-new-tabs-automatically-2-overview.webp "Prevent Websites From Opening New Tabs Automatically 2 Overview")


There are several reasons why websites open new tabs automatically. Some common reasons include:

- Malicious scripts or malware: Some websites may contain malicious scripts that open new tabs automatically to spread malware or viruses.
- Ads and pop-ups: Websites may use ads or pop-ups to open new tabs automatically, often to promote products or services.
- Website design: Some websites may open new tabs automatically as part of their design, such as when you click on a link or button.

## How to Prevent Websites from Opening New Tabs Automatically

To **prevent websites from opening new tabs automatically**, you can try the following methods:

1. Disable JavaScript: Disabling JavaScript can prevent websites from opening new tabs automatically. However, this may also affect the functionality of some websites.
2. Use a pop-up blocker: Pop-up blockers can help block automatically opening tabs. You can use a built-in pop-up blocker in your browser or install a third-party extension.
3. Install an ad blocker: Ad blockers can help block ads that open new tabs automatically. You can use a built-in ad blocker in your browser or install a third-party extension.
