## What are Trackers?

![Protect Your Online Privacy Overview](/content/images/protect-your-online-privacy/protect-your-online-privacy-overview.webp "Protect Your Online Privacy Overview")


Trackers are scripts or cookies that websites use to collect information about your online activities, such as browsing history, search queries, and personal data. These trackers can be used for various purposes, including targeted advertising, analytics, and data collection. While some trackers may be harmless, others can pose a significant threat to your online privacy and security.

### Types of Trackers

There are several types of trackers, including:

- Cookies: Small text files stored on your device to track your browsing history and preferences.
- Scripts: JavaScript code that runs on your device to collect information about your online activities.
- Beacons: Small images or scripts that track your online behavior and report back to the tracker.

## How Do Trackers Work?

Trackers work by collecting information about your online activities and sending it back to the tracker's server. This information can include:

- Browsing history: The websites you visit and the pages you view.
- Search queries: The searches you perform and the results you click on.
- Personal data: Your name, email address, and other personal information.

Trackers can also use advanced techniques, such as fingerprinting, to collect information about your device and browser.

## Why Block Trackers?

![Protect Your Online Privacy Features](/content/images/protect-your-online-privacy/protect-your-online-privacy-features.webp "Protect Your Online Privacy Features")

- Improve your online security: Reduce the risk of malware and other online threats by blocking trackers.
