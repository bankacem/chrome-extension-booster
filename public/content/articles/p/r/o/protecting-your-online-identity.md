## What are Trackers and Why are They a Threat?

Trackers are small pieces of code that websites use to collect information about your online activities. They can be used to track your browsing habits, location, and even personal data. While some trackers are harmless, others can be used for malicious purposes, such as identity theft or targeted advertising. A **Chrome extension for blocking trackers** can help prevent these trackers from collecting your data, keeping your online identity safe.

### Types of Trackers

There are several types of trackers that can be used to collect your data, including:

- Cookies: small text files that are stored on your device to track your browsing habits
- Scripts: pieces of code that are used to collect data about your online activities
- Pixels: small images that are used to track your location and browsing habits
