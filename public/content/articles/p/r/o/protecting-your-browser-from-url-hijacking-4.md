## What is URL Hijacking?

URL hijacking, also known as URL redirection or URL spoofing, is a malicious technique used by attackers to redirect users from a legitimate website to a fake or malicious one. This can be achieved through various means, including phishing emails, infected software, or compromised websites. When a user clicks on a hijacked URL, they may be taken to a website that appears to be the real deal but is actually a phishing site designed to steal sensitive information or install malware.

### How URL Hijacking Works

The process of URL hijacking typically involves the following steps:

- Malicious actors compromise a website or create a fake one that mimics a legitimate site.
- The attackers then manipulate the URL of the compromised or fake website to redirect users to the malicious site.
- When a user clicks on the hijacked URL, they are redirected to the malicious site, where they may be prompted to enter sensitive information or download malware.

## Types of URL Hijacking

![Protecting Your Browser From Url Hijacking 4 Overview](/content/images/protecting-your-browser-from-url-hijacking-4/protecting-your-browser-from-url-hijacking-4-overview.webp "Protecting Your Browser From Url Hijacking 4 Overview")


There are several types of URL hijacking, including:

1. **Phishing-based URL hijacking**: This type of hijacking involves using phishing emails or messages to trick users into clicking on a hijacked URL.
2. **Malware-based URL hijacking**: This type of hijacking involves infecting a user's device with malware that redirects them to a malicious website.
3. **Browser hijacking**: This type of hijacking involves modifying a user's browser settings to redirect them to a malicious website.

## Consequences of URL Hijacking

The consequences of URL hijacking can be severe, including:

- **Financial loss**: Users may be tricked into revealing financial information, such as credit card numbers or bank account details.
- **Identity theft**: Users may be tricked into revealing personal information, such as social security numbers or addresses.
- **Malware infection**: Users may be tricked into [downloading](/blog/the-best-chrome-plugins-for-downloading-media "The Definitive Guide to the Best Chrome Plugins for Media Downloading in 2025") malware, which can compromise their device and steal sensitive information.

2. **Be cautious with links**: Avoid clicking on suspicious links or URLs, especially those from unknown sources.
3. **Use a browser extension**: Consider using a browser extension like [Redirect Shield](/extension/redirect-shield) to block malicious redirects.

### Additional Tips

In addition to the above best practices, consider the following tips to **protect your browser from URL hijacking**:
