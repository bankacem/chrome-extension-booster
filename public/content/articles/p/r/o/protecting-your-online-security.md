## What are Malicious Websites?

Malicious websites are online platforms designed to harm your device or steal your personal data. These websites can be infected with malware, viruses, or other types of cyber threats. Visiting a malicious website can lead to various problems, including data theft, identity theft, and device damage.

Some common types of malicious websites include:

- Phishing websites: Designed to steal your personal data, such as login credentials or financial information.
- Malware websites: Infected with viruses or other types of malware that can harm your device.
- Scam websites: Fake online platforms that promise unrealistic offers or services.
