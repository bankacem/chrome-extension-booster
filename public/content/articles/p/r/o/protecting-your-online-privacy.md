### Types of Trackers

There are several types of trackers that can be used to collect your data. These include:

- Cookies: Small text files stored on your device to track your browsing history and preferences.
- Pixels: Tiny images embedded in web pages to track your online activities.
- Fingerprinting scripts: Code used to collect information about your device's hardware and software configurations.
- Beacons: Small files used to track your interactions with web pages and applications.

### How Trackers Work

Trackers work by collecting data about your online activities and transmitting it to third-party servers. This data can then be used to create detailed profiles about your interests, preferences, and behaviors. By using a **Chrome extension to detect trackers**, you can prevent this data from being collected and transmitted, ensuring a more private and secure browsing experience.
