<p><strong>Privacy Badger</strong> is a free and open-source browser extension developed by the Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF). It works by automatically learning which domains are tracking you as you browse the web, and then blocks those trackers. On the other hand, <strong>Ghostery</strong> is a popular extension that also blocks trackers, but with a more comprehensive approach, including the ability to block ads, scripts, and other potentially harmful content.</p>

<h3>Key Features of Privacy Badger</h3>

<p>Some of the key features of <strong>Privacy Badger</strong> include:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Automatic tracker blocking: <strong>Privacy Badger</strong> learns which domains are tracking you and blocks them automatically.</li>
  <li>Anti-fingerprinting: <strong>Privacy Badger</strong> also blocks fingerprinting scripts that can be used to identify your browser and device.</li>
  <li>Open-source: <strong>Privacy Badger</strong> is completely open-source, which means that its code is transparent and can be reviewed by anyone.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Key Features of Ghostery</h3>

<p>Some of the key features of <strong>Ghostery</strong> include:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Comprehensive tracker blocking: <strong>Ghostery</strong> blocks a wide range of trackers, including ads, scripts, and other potentially harmful content.</li>
  <li>Customizable blocking options: <strong>Ghostery</strong> allows you to customize which types of trackers to block, giving you more control over your online privacy.</li>
  <li>Anti-malware protection: <strong>Ghostery</strong> also includes anti-malware protection, which can help protect your device from malicious software.</li>
</ul>
