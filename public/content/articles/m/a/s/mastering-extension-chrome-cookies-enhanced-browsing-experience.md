## What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device to collect data about your browsing habits, preferences, and history. They are widely used for various purposes, including personalizing your browsing experience, tracking your activities, and even targeting you with specific advertisements. While cookies can enhance your online experience, they also raise significant privacy concerns, as they can potentially be used to track your movements across the web.

### Types of Cookies

There are several types of cookies, each serving a different purpose. These include:

- Session cookies: Temporary cookies that are deleted when you close your browser.
- Persistent cookies: Cookies that remain on your device until they expire or are manually deleted.
- First-party cookies: Cookies set by the website you are visiting.
- Third-party cookies: Cookies set by external services or advertisers.

## How Do Cookies Work?

Cookies work by storing a unique identifier on your device, which is then used to recognize you when you return to a website. This allows websites to remember your preferences, login details, and other information, providing a more personalized experience. However, this also means that cookies can be used to track your movements across the web, potentially compromising your privacy.

### Cookie Lifecycle

The lifecycle of a cookie typically involves the following stages:

1. A website sets a cookie on your device.
2. The cookie is stored on your device until it expires or is deleted.
3. When you return to the website, the cookie is retrieved and used to recognize you.
