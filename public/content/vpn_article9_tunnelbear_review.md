## What Is TunnelBear? (The Friendly Canadian)

TunnelBear is a VPN service founded in 2011 in Toronto, Canada. It was acquired by McAfee (now part of Intel) in 2018, then spun off when McAfee was acquired by Symphony Technology Group.

In 2023, TunnelBear was acquired by **Kape Technologies** (now known as Private Internet), the same company that owns CyberGhost, Private Internet Access, and ZenMate.

### The Company Behind the Bear

| Entity | Role |
|--------|------|
| **TunnelBear** | Original brand (founded 2011) |
| **McAfee** | Former owner (2018-2021) |
| **Kape Technologies** | Current owner (2023-present) |
| **Location** | Toronto, Ontario, Canada |

**The Canadian jurisdiction is a concern.** Canada is part of Five Eyes, Nine Eyes, and Fourteen Eyes surveillance alliances. While TunnelBear claims no-logs, the legal environment is less privacy-friendly than Panama (NordVPN) or Switzerland (ProtonVPN).

### The Bear Brand

TunnelBear's entire identity revolves around being friendly and approachable:
- **Bear mascot:** Appears throughout the app and website
- **Simple language:** No technical jargon
- **Visual feedback:** Bear digs a tunnel when connecting, roars when connected
- **Transparency reports:** Published annually (rare for VPNs)
- **Independent audits:** Annual security audits by Cure53

**This branding works.** TunnelBear is the VPN I recommend to my parents, my non-technical friends, and anyone intimidated by technology. It's genuinely delightful to use.

## Speed Test Results: Slow but Steady

TunnelBear is consistently the slowest major VPN we tested. Here's the data:

| Server | Download | Upload | Latency | Notes |
|--------|----------|--------|---------|-------|
| **US (East)** | 198 Mbps | 156 Mbps | +24ms | Best performance |
| **US (West)** | 178 Mbps | 145 Mbps | +31ms | Slow |
| **UK** | 156 Mbps | 134 Mbps | +38ms | Slow |
| **Germany** | 167 Mbps | 145 Mbps | +35ms | Slow |
| **Japan** | 123 Mbps | 98 Mbps | +67ms | Very slow |
| **Australia** | 89 Mbps | 67 Mbps | +112ms | Painfully slow |
| **Average** | **178 Mbps** | **145 Mbps** | **+45ms** | **Slowest tested** |

### Why Is TunnelBear So Slow?

1. **Smaller server network:** ~3,000 servers vs NordVPN's 8,900+
2. **Less bandwidth per server:** More users per server
3. **No specialized protocols:** Uses standard WireGuard/OpenVPN without optimization
4. **No 10 Gbps servers:** Many competitors upgraded to faster infrastructure

**The speed is usable for browsing** but frustrating for:
- 4K streaming (needs 25 Mbps, TunnelBear sometimes struggles)
- Large downloads (takes 3-4x longer than NordVPN)
- Video calls (occasional quality drops)
- Gaming (high latency + packet loss)

## Privacy & Security: Audited and Transparent

### The Good: Transparency and Audits

TunnelBear does privacy right in several ways:

**Annual Security Audits:**
- Audited by **Cure53** every year since 2017
- Full source code review
- Infrastructure penetration testing
- Results published publicly

**Transparency Reports:**
- Published annually
- Discloses government data requests
- Shows how many requests received and complied with
- Rare level of openness in the VPN industry

**Security Features:**
| Feature | Implementation |
|---------|---------------|
| **Encryption** | AES-256 |
| **Protocols** | WireGuard, OpenVPN, IKEv2 |
| **Kill switch** | VigilantBear (Premium only) |
| **Split tunneling** | SplitBear (Premium only) |
| **GhostBear** | Obfuscation to hide VPN traffic |
| **No-logs policy** | Claims no logs, audited |

### The Bad: Canadian Jurisdiction

**Canada is part of Five Eyes.** This means:
- Canadian intelligence agencies cooperate with US, UK, Australia, and NZ
- Legal frameworks allow data sharing between member countries
- TunnelBear could be compelled to log data under Canadian law
- Even if they don't log now, legal pressure could change that

**TunnelBear's response:** They claim their no-logs policy means they have no data to hand over. But unlike ProtonVPN (court-tested) or NordVPN (Panama-based), this claim hasn't been tested in a Canadian court.

- You value **transparency reports** and corporate openness
- You need a VPN for **basic browsing privacy** only

## FAQ

### Is TunnelBear good for beginners?
Yes. TunnelBear is the most beginner-friendly VPN available. The interface is intuitive, the bear animations provide delightful feedback, and there's no technical jargon. If you've never used a VPN before, TunnelBear is the perfect starting point.

### Does TunnelBear unblock Netflix?
No. TunnelBear intentionally doesn't try to unblock streaming services. Their focus is on privacy and security, not geo-unblocking. For Netflix, use NordVPN or ExpressVPN.

### Is TunnelBear's free tier enough?
No. 2 GB/month is roughly 2 hours of 720p YouTube or light browsing for a week. It's sufficient to test the service but inadequate for daily use. For genuinely free unlimited VPN, use ProtonVPN's free tier.

### Why is TunnelBear so slow?
TunnelBear has a smaller server network (~3,000 servers) and less bandwidth per server than competitors. They also lack optimized protocols like NordLynx or Lightway. The result is consistently slower speeds — usable for browsing but frustrating for downloads and streaming.

### Is TunnelBear safe for torrenting?
No. TunnelBear doesn't support P2P traffic on any server. Even if you try, the connection will be blocked. For torrenting, use NordVPN, Surfshark, or ProtonVPN Plus.

### Does TunnelBear keep logs?
TunnelBear claims a strict no-logs policy and has been audited by Cure53 annually since 2017. However, they're based in Canada (Five Eyes), which is less privacy-friendly than Panama or Switzerland. Their no-logs claim hasn't been tested in court like ProtonVPN's.

### Is TunnelBear owned by a trustworthy company?
TunnelBear is owned by Kape Technologies (formerly Crossrider), a company with a controversial history. Kape previously developed ad injection software and browser toolbars considered malware by some security researchers. While TunnelBear operates independently, the parent company's history concerns some privacy advocates.

### What's GhostBear and when should I use it?
GhostBear is TunnelBear's obfuscation feature that makes VPN traffic look like regular HTTPS traffic. Use it in countries or networks that block VPN connections (some workplaces, schools, or restrictive countries). It slightly reduces speeds but improves connectivity.

### Can I use TunnelBear on multiple devices?
Premium users get unlimited simultaneous connections. Free users are limited to one device at a time. The Chrome extension works alongside the desktop and mobile apps.

### Is TunnelBear better than ProtonVPN?
For beginners and ease of use: yes. For privacy, speed, features, and free tier: no. ProtonVPN offers unlimited free data, Swiss jurisdiction, faster speeds, and more features. TunnelBear wins only on user-friendliness.
